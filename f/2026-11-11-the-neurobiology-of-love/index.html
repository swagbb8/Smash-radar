Is your love actually a form of addiction?

File no. 0269November 11, 2026Human relationships

Romantic love shares symptoms with behavioral addiction.

In early stages, intense love shows symptoms like euphoria, craving, tolerance, and withdrawal. Researchers have not officially categorized it as a chemical or behavioral addiction.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. The oxytocin (OT) system is a critical factor for the development of addiction.

    “It seems that the OT system is a critical factor for the development of addiction.”

    Review, 2016. Zou et al., Frontiers in Psychology.

  2. People in the early stages of intense romantic love show symptoms of substance and behavioral addictions, such as euphoria, craving, tolerance, emotional and physical dependence, withdrawal and relapse.

    “Individuals in the early stage of intense romantic love show many symptoms of substance and non-substance or behavioral addictions, including euphoria, craving, tolerance, emotional and physical dependence, withdrawal and relapse.”

    Review, 2016. Fisher et al., Frontiers in Psychology.

  3. Social attachment may be understood as a behavioral addiction where a subject becomes addicted to another individual and the cues that predict social reward.

    “Social attachment may be understood as a behavioral addiction, whereby the subject becomes addicted to another individual and the cues that predict social reward.”

    Review, 2012. Burkett & Young, Psychopharmacology.

  4. Passionate love is linked to the activation of the bilateral ventral tegmental area (VTA).

    “The ALE results showed significant activation of the brain regions in the left ventral tegmental area (VTA), right thalamus, left substantia nigra, and the left putamen for maternal love, but in the bilateral VTA for passionate love.”

    Meta-analysis, 2022. Shih et al., Brain Sciences.

  5. The functional alterations in reward and emotion regulation network and the DA system may be the neurophysiological basis of romantic love as a behavioral addiction.

    “These findings indicate that the functional alterations in reward and emotion regulation network and the DA system may be the neurophysiological basis of romantic love as a behavioral addiction”

    Review, 2016. Zou et al., Frontiers in Psychology.

  6. The neuropeptides oxytocin and vasopressin are hypothesized to integrate social information into attachment processes that is not present in drug addiction.

    “The neuropeptides oxytocin and vasopressin are hypothesized to integrate social information into attachment processes that is not present in drug addiction.”

    Review, 2012. Burkett & Young, Psychopharmacology.

The brain uses the same pathways for love and drugs.

Passionate love activates the ventral tegmental area. Functional alterations in reward and emotion regulation networks may form the neurophysiological basis of love as a behavioral addiction.

Your attachment is a neurological loop.

The oxytocin system is critical for addiction. That may be why you feel physical dependence on another person. It is a social reward system wired for repetition.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that romantic love shares many neurobiological mechanisms and symptoms with addiction, but it also possesses unique social and evolutionary components.

The sources note that while symptoms overlap, researchers have not officially categorized romantic love as a chemical or behavioral addiction.
