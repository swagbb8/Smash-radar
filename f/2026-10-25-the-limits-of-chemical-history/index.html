Organophosphorus chemistry advanced in the late 20th century.

The evidence shows rapid progress in these compounds occurred in the second half of the 20th century.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. Chlorine-loaded MOF/fiber composites can detoxify sulfur-mustard-based chemical warfare agents and simulants within minutes of exposure.

    “We then created chlorine-loaded MOF/fiber composites that act as biocidal and reactive textiles that are capable of not only detoxifying sulfur-mustard-based chemical warfare agents and simulants”

    Study, 2023. Ma et al., Accounts of Materials Research.

  2. Filtration materials currently in common use were designed before organophosphorus compounds were used as chemical weapons.

    “The filtration materials currently in common use for protection against chemical warfare agents were designed before organophosphorus compounds were used as chemical weapons.”

    Study, 2017. Trotochaud et al., Chemistry of Materials.

  3. The USSR developed the FOLIANT and NOVICHOK programs as a reaction to the English/American invention of the VX agent.

    “Both programs were developed by the USSR as a reaction to English/American invention of VX agent.”

    Review, 2018. Nepovimová & Kuča, Food and Chemical Toxicology.

  4. Organophosphorus compounds have been used as chemical warfare agents in both military operations and for terrorist purposes.

    “In addition to commercial and peaceful applications, they were used as chemical warfare agents (CWA), both in military operations and for terrorist purposes.”

    Study, 2025. Boczkowski et al., Molecules.

  5. Rapid progress in organophosphorus chemistry occurred in the second half of the 20th century.

    “Rapid progress in organophosphorus chemistry in the second half of the 20th century is also described.”

    Study, 2025. Boczkowski et al., Molecules.
