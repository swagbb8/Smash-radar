Your brain is being hijacked

Is dopamine about pleasure?

File no. 0042October 8, 2026The human brain

Dopamine signals reward prediction errors.

Your brain calculates the difference between what you expected to get and what you actually received.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Dopamine levels can ramp gradually as a goal is approached.

    “gradually ramping stratal dopamine levels as a goal is approached.”

    Experiment, 2013. Gershman, Neural Computation.

  2. The dopamine signal increases nonlinearly with reward value and codes formal economic utility.

    “The dopamine signal increases nonlinearly with reward value and codes formal economic utility.”

    Study, 2016. Schultz, Dialogues in Clinical Neuroscience.

  3. Dopamine neurons reflect the integrated subjective value of different reward attributes like amount, risk, and type.

    “The prediction error responses of single dopamine neurons reflected the integrated subjective value inferred from the choices, rather than the singular reward attributes.”

    Experiment, 2014. Lak et al., Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences.

  4. Dopamine neurons provide a global mechanism for synaptic modification that underlies a specific class of reinforcement learning mechanisms.

    “the phasic activity of the midbrain dopamine neurons provides a global mechanism for synaptic modification. These synaptic modifications, in turn, provide the mechanistic underpinning for a specific class of reinforcement learning mechanisms”

    Experiment, 2011. Glimcher, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences.

  5. Dopamine neurons signal a reward prediction error, which is the difference between received and predicted rewards.

    “Reward prediction errors consist of the differences between received and predicted rewards.”

    Study, 2016. Schultz, Dialogues in Clinical Neuroscience.

  6. Dopamine neurons are activated by more reward than predicted and show depressed activity with less reward than predicted.

    “they are activated by more reward than predicted (positive prediction error), remain at baseline activity for fully predicted rewards, and show depressed activity with less reward than predicted (negative prediction error).”

    Study, 2016. Schultz, Dialogues in Clinical Neuroscience.

It codes your subjective value.

Dopamine neurons reflect the integrated value of reward attributes like amount, risk, and type. They provide a mechanism for the learning that drives your reward-seeking behavior.

The drive is the calculation.

That may be why you feel a constant push toward a goal. Your brain is not just experiencing a reward; it is calculating the utility of the next step.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The scroll.

You swipe on your phone in a dark room. Each new post is a calculation of whether the next bit of information is worth your attention.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that dopamine is a mechanism for calculating reward prediction errors and encoding subjective value to facilitate learning and reward-seeking behavior.

The sources do not explicitly distinguish between the sensation of pleasure and the drive of wanting, focusing instead on the calculation of reward prediction errors.

Are you seeking the reward or the calculation?
