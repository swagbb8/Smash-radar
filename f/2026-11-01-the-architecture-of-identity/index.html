Does your belief system limit your learning?

File no. 0215November 1, 2026The human brain

Your brain uses domain-general systems to inhibit imitation.

The inhibition of imitation relies more on a domain-general selection system than a system specifically for social cognition.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. The inhibition of imitation relies to a greater extent on a domain-general selection system rather than a domain-specific system that supports social cognition.

    “the inhibition of imitation relies to a greater extent on a domain-general selection system rather than a domain-specific system that supports social cognition.”

    Experiment, 2018. Darda et al., Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience.

  2. The removal of a contact point rarely results in the total elimination of facial pain.

    “The removal of a contact point rarely results in the total elimination of facial pain making the theory that a contact point is responsible unlikely.”

    Systematic review, 2013. Harrison & Jones, Clinical Otolaryngology. This shows a link, not a cause.

  3. The same neuroplastic mechanisms that create performance-based identity can also facilitate reconstruction of the authentic self.

    “Ultimately, the paper argues that the same neuroplastic mechanisms that create performance-based identity can also facilitate reconstruction of the authentic self.”

    Study, 2026. Dauch), Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research).

  4. The presence of a contact point is not a good predictor of facial pain.

    “The presence of a contact point is not a good predictor of facial pain.”

    Systematic review, 2013. Harrison & Jones, Clinical Otolaryngology.

  5. CBT for OCD was associated with both clinical improvement and enhanced neural indices of response inhibition, as reflected by increased No-Go P3 amplitude.

    “CBT for OCD was associated with both clinical improvement and enhanced neural indices of response inhibition, as reflected by increased No-Go P3 amplitude.”

    Randomized trial, 2026. Çınaroğlu et al., BMC psychology.

  6. The backward inhibitory effect reflects the process of overcoming residual inhibition from a recently performed task.

    “This difference is called the backward inhibitory effect (BI effect), which reflects the process of overcoming residual inhibition from a recently performed task (i.e., deinhibition).”

    Review, 2022. Chen et al., Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience.

Neural pathways can be reconstructed.

The same neuroplastic mechanisms that create performance-based identity can also facilitate the reconstruction of the authentic self. Therapy can enhance neural indices of response inhibition.

Identity is a structural process.

That may be why your identity feels fixed. The mechanisms for inhibition and identity reconstruction are complex systems rather than just simple belief-based pathways.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The honest summary

The sources show that neural pathways can be modified through therapy and neuroplasticity, but they do not provide direct evidence that current beliefs actively inhibit the acquisition of contradictory information.

The sources provide various mechanisms for inhibition and neuroplasticity but lack a specific study directly measuring the inhibition of contradictory information based on existing beliefs.
