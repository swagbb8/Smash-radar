The future is closer than you think

You think experts have the edge. The data says otherwise.

File no. 0039October 8, 2026The future

AI narrows the gap between skilled and unskilled workers.

Generative AI consistently boosts productivity and quality for lower-skilled or less experienced workers while reducing inequality.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence backs this.

What the research found

  1. ChatGPT reduced inequality between workers during writing tasks.

    “Inequality between workers decreased, and concern and excitement about AI temporarily rose.”

    Experiment, 2023. Noy & Zhang, Science (New York, N.Y.).

  2. ChatGPT's fundamental errors may pose a special challenge for non-experts.

    “In light of observed limitations, and given that the tool's fundamental errors may pose a special challenge for non-experts, ChatGPT should be used with a strategic methodology.”

    Experiment, 2023. Azaria et al., Data Intelligence.

  3. ChatGPT reduced the average time taken for midlevel professional writing tasks by 40% and increased output quality by 18%.

    “The average time taken decreased by 40% and output quality rose by 18%.”

    Experiment, 2023. Noy & Zhang, Science (New York, N.Y.).

  4. GenAI causes larger gains for less experienced consumers in online retail.

    “Finally, we document substantial demand-side heterogeneity, with larger gains for less experienced consumers.”

    Experiment, 2026. Fang et al., CESifo.

  5. Access to generative AI ideas causes stories to be evaluated as more creative, better written, and more enjoyable, especially among less creative writers.

    “We find that access to generative AI ideas causes stories to be evaluated as more creative, better written, and more enjoyable, especially among less creative writers.”

    Experiment, 2024. Doshi & Hauser, Science advances.

  6. GitHub Copilot caused developers at Microsoft to complete 12.92% to 21.83% more pull requests per week and 7.51% to 8.69% more at Accenture.

    “our preliminary results provide suggestive evidence that these developers became more productive, completing 12.92% to 21.83% more pull requests per week at Microsoft and 7.51% to 8.69% at Accenture”

    Experiment, 2024. Cui et al..

The tools level the playing field.

ChatGPT reduces inequality in writing tasks. It causes larger gains for less experienced consumers in online retail and makes stories more creative for less creative writers.

The cost of easier output.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that generative AI consistently boosts productivity and quality for lower-skilled or less experienced workers while reducing inequality.

While individual productivity and creativity increase, the collective diversity of novel content may be reduced.
