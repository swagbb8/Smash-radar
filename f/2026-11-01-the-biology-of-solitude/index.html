Does your solitude cause physical inflammation?

File no. 0214November 1, 2026Science and reality

Social isolation reduces certain inflammation markers.

In some models, prolonged isolation from weaning reduced IL-6 and its mRNA expression in the prefrontal cortex. This finding is mixed and contested.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. In rats, prolonged periods of social isolation from weaning reduced IL-6 and its mRNA expression in the prefrontal cortex.

    “along with decreased IL-6 and its mRNA expression in the prefrontal cortex.”

    Study, 2019. Zuelli et al., Frontiers in Neuroscience.

  2. Prolonged periods of social isolation from weaning reduces the anti-inflammatory cytokine IL-10 protein in the blood and the hippocampus of rats.

    “Prolonged pwSI decreased IL-10 protein and mRNA in the blood, and IL-10 protein in the hippocampus”

    Study, 2019. Zuelli et al., Frontiers in Neuroscience.

  3. Repeated and chronic social stress is linked to upregulated proinflammatory gene expression and oxidative stress.

    “Repeated and chronic social stress leads to glucocorticoid resistance, enhanced myelopoiesis, upregulated proinflammatory gene expression, and oxidative stress.”

    Review, 2017. Xia & Li, Antioxidants and Redox Signaling.

  4. Caregivers' perceived stress and perceived loneliness may form a symptom cluster that is linked to risks for physical and mental illness.

    “Thus, caregivers' perceived stress and perceived loneliness may form a symptom cluster that can serve as a marker of risks for physical and mental illness.”

    Study, 2018. Kovaleva et al., Journal of Psychosocial Nursing and Mental Health Services.

  5. Social stress is linked to the development of psychological problems ranging from exaggerated anxiety and depression to antisocial and violence-related behaviors.

    “Social stress can lead to the development of psychological problems ranging from exaggerated anxiety and depression to antisocial and violence-related behaviors.”

    Review, 2018. Takahashi et al., Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience.

  6. Social isolation in mice is linked to substantive cytokine responses in the hippocampus.

    “We also found substantive cytokine responses to social isolation in the hippocampus; housing and genotype interaction effects were significant (by 2‐Factor ANOVA) for 15 cytokines”

    Experiment, 2019. Guttmann et al., The FASEB Journal.

Isolation affects your internal chemistry.

Social stress is linked to upregulated proinflammatory gene expression and oxidative stress. However, isolation also reduces anti-inflammatory cytokines like IL-10 in the blood and hippocampus.

Your solitude changes your biology.

The way you interact with others shapes your physical state. That may be why loneliness is linked to a cluster of risks for physical and mental illness.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that social stress and isolation are linked to inflammatory markers and oxidative stress, but the specific patterns of these cytokines can vary.

The causal role of these molecular mechanisms in the development of loneliness-associated cardiovascular disease remains unclear.
