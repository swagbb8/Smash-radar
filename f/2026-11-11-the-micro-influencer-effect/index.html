Relatability is not enough to influence you.

Micro-influencers only change your behavior when they drive active brand engagement. Trust alone does not guarantee a purchase.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Micro-influencer campaigns work most effectively when they encourage active brand engagement rather than depending only on perceived attractiveness, trustworthiness, or parasocial familiarity.

    “the condensed paper therefore argues that micro-influencer campaigns work most effectively when they encourage active brand engagement rather than depending only on perceived attractiveness, trustworthiness, or parasocial familiarity.”

    Observational study, 2026. John, INTERANTIONAL JOURNAL OF SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH IN ENGINEERING AND MANAGEMENT.

  2. Micro-influencers who are believed to be genuine and more trustworthy affect consumer buying decisions more than macro-influencers.

    “It is found that micro-influencers who are believed to be genuine and more trustworthy, affect consumer buying decisions more than macro-influencers who have a wide reach but may not be seen as genuine enough.”

    Study, 2024. Chen, Advances in Economics Management and Political Sciences.

  3. Micro-influencers significantly impact consumer decision-making by leveraging their relatability and perceived authenticity to foster deeper emotional connections.

    “Findings reveal that micro-influencers significantly impact consumer decision-making, leveraging their relatability and perceived authenticity to foster deeper emotional connections.”

    Study, 2025. LAKSHMI et al..

  4. Online interaction with a brand because of micro-influencer endorsements is linked to purchase intention.

    “one variable emerges as significant: online interaction with a brand because of micro-influencer endorsements positively predicts purchase intention.”

    Observational study, 2026. John, INTERANTIONAL JOURNAL OF SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH IN ENGINEERING AND MANAGEMENT. This shows a link, not a cause.

  5. Micro-influencers differ significantly from others in terms of their interaction power and perceived high credibility in social media marketing.

    “It has been concluded that micro-influencers differ significantly from others in terms of their interaction power and perceived high credibility in social media marketing.”

    Review, 2025. Kuzoren et al., Journal of Global Business Insights.

The power of the micro-influencer.

Micro-influencers use perceived authenticity to create emotional connections. They have more interaction power and higher credibility than macro-influencers in social media marketing.

The mechanism of influence.

That may be why you feel a personal connection to smaller creators. They are designed to feel like friends, making their influence on your buying decisions more effective than celebrities.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A scroll in the quiet.

You sit on your sofa at night. You watch a person you recognize as a peer share a product. You feel a connection. You click a link.

The honest summary

The evidence confirms that micro-influencers are more effective than macro-influencers at driving consumer behavior due to perceived authenticity and interaction power.

The research is limited by small sample sizes, a focus on specific social media platforms, and a lack of theoretical integration across different platforms.
