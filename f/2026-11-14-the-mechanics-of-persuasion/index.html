Who is actually changing your mind?

Algorithms mediate deliberate data-driven persuasion.

Evidence confirms that algorithms can facilitate persuasion. However, there is no evidence that models can predict your conversions before you are conscious of them.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Persuasion in the direction of information occurs regardless of background characteristics, initial beliefs, or ideological position.

    “Third, persuasion in the direction of information occurs regardless of background characteristics, initial beliefs, or ideological position.”

    Experiment, 2016. Coppock.

  2. People are more easily persuaded by posts when they experience information overload.

    “The interaction effect revealed that in both positive and negative posts, less reading time (i. e. information overload) had a stronger influence.”

    Experiment, 2021. Wang et al., Frontiers in Psychology.

  3. In a positive information overload condition, the attitudes of individuals eventually change in a positive way.

    “Therefore, in the positive information overload condition, the attitudes of individuals eventually change in a positive way.”

    Experiment, 2021. Wang et al., Frontiers in Psychology.

  4. Online social media news leads individuals to be exposed to fake news, which is linked to higher levels of political discussion, ultimately facilitating political persuasion.

    “structural equation tests reveal that online and social media news lead individuals to be exposed to fake news, which, in turn, predict higher levels of political discussion, ultimately facilitating political persuasion in the social media realm.”

    Observational study, 2022. Zúñiga et al., American Behavioral Scientist.

  5. Changes in political attitudes are not ephemeral, in most cases lasting at least 10 days.

    “Finally, these changes in political attitudes are not ephemeral, in most cases lasting at least 10 days.”

    Experiment, 2016. Coppock.

  6. Algorithmic persuasion involves deliberate persuasive attempts that are data-driven and mediated by algorithms.

    “Algorithmic persuasion involves deliberate persuasive attempts that are data-driven and mediated by algorithms.”

    Study, 2024. Noort et al., Amsterdam University Press eBooks.

Information overload makes you more susceptible.

When you experience a high volume of information, you are more easily persuaded. In positive conditions, these attitudes eventually change in a positive way.

Your views update based on input.

Persuasion may follow a predictable pattern of updating your views based on new information rather than specific psychological vulnerabilities. This process occurs regardless of your initial beliefs.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A late night scroll through your feed.

You sit in a dark room. You see a steady stream of news. You feel tired. Your perspective shifts slightly with every post you read.

The honest summary

The sources confirm that algorithms can mediate persuasion and that information overload can increase its effectiveness, but they do not specifically prove that models can predict conversions before they are conscious.

The sources do not provide specific evidence regarding the ability of machine learning models to identify latent ideological shifts or psychological vulnerabilities before a user's self-reported change.
