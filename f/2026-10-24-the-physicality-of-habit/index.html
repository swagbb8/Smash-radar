What the research found

  1. Significant changes in brain activation, structure, microstructure, and structural and functional connectivity were reported with different types of trainings in the majority (87%) of the studies.

    “Significant changes in brain activation, structure, microstructure, and structural and functional connectivity were reported with different types of trainings in the majority (87%) of the studies.”

    Systematic review, 2021. Tymofiyeva & Gaschler, Frontiers in Human Neuroscience.

  2. Early life stress (ELS) has a significant impact on the structural and functional plasticity of the mPFC in adolescents.

  3. Our finding reveals a time window of plasticity occurring during late childhood which may accommodate crucial changes during puberty and the new developmental tasks that an adolescent faces.

    Study, 2012. Khundrakpam et al., Cerebral Cortex.

    “Significant correlation of performance improvement with neural changes was reported in 51% of the studies.”

    Systematic review, 2021. Tymofiyeva & Gaschler, Frontiers in Human Neuroscience.

    “CONCLUSION: Chronic cannabis use may alter brain structure and function in adult and adolescent population.”

Neuroplasticity is a constant process.

The brain shows structural and functional plasticity in response to training, stress, and substance use. These changes can occur during specific windows of development, such as late childhood and puberty.
