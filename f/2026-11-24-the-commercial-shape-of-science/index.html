The uncomfortable truth

Who decided what your medicine does?

File no. 0327November 24, 2026History

Commercial interests have shaped anthropology for nearly a century.

Business interests have influenced the development of the field of anthropology. They also shape the types of pharmaceutical effects that are made and remade for society.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Pharmaceuticals are not “discovered”; they are made and remade in relation to shifting contexts.

    “We show that there is no pure (pharmaceutical) object that precedes its socialization. Pharmaceuticals are not “discovered”; they are made and remade in relation to shifting contexts.”

    Review, 2017. Hardon & Sanabria, Annual Review of Anthropology.

  2. Prominent academic researchers may become intellectual “suppliers” to businesses.

    “Over time, this field has attracted prominent academic researchers (as will be discussed herein), who may become intellectual “suppliers” to businesses, and thus part of the field.”

    Study, 2012. Baba, Journal of Business Anthropology.

  3. Corporations and other commercial interests are a distal, structural, societal factor that causes disease and injury.

    “we propose a framework to systematically study corporations and other commercial interests as a distal, structural, societal factor that causes disease and injury.”

    Review, 2018. Lima & Galea, Globalization and Health. This shows a link, not a cause.

  4. The nation-state is complicated by the emergence to dominance of state and state-like oligarchic-corporate state formations.

    “the political environment of the nation-state is complicated by the emergence to dominance of state and state-like oligarchic-corporate state formations.”

    Study, 2005. Kapferer, Anthropological Theory.

  5. The field of anthropology has been influenced by the domain of business for the better part of a century.

    “the expansive domain of business... has influenced the development or “making” of anthropology as a discipline and a profession for the better part of a century”

    Study, 2012. Baba, Journal of Business Anthropology.

  6. Commercial interests and societal concerns shape the kinds of pharmaceutical effects that are actualized.

    “This literature foregrounds insights into how commercial interests and societal concerns shape the kinds of pharmaceutical effects that are actualized”

    Review, 2017. Hardon & Sanabria, Annual Review of Anthropology.

The evidence is mixed on specific historical funding.

While research shows a link between commercial interests and societal health, there is no data provided on the specific funding of early biological studies for labor exploitation.

That may be why your health is a commodity.

If corporations are structural factors in disease and academic researchers serve as suppliers to business, your physical well-being is tied to a commercial cycle.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

You pick up a bottle of medicine at the pharmacy.

You look at the label and the price. You assume it is a neutral cure for a physical problem.

The honest summary

The evidence confirms that commercial interests influence anthropological development and pharmaceutical production, but it lacks specific data on the funding of early biological studies for labor exploitation.

None of the provided sources contain any information regarding the specific history of 'scientific' racism or the history of labor exploitation.
