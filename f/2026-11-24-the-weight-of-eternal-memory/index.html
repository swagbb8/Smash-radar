Does your past have more power than your present?

File no. 0325November 24, 2026The future

Your attention flows more toward the past than the present.

The secondary flow of attention to past events generated by remembering processes is larger than the primary attention flow to the current event.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. The secondary flow of attention to past events generated by remembering processes is larger than the primary attention flow to the current event.

    “We show that, on average, the secondary flow of attention to past events generated by these remembering processes is larger than the primary attention flow to the current event.”

    Study, 2017. García-Gavilanes et al., Science Advances.

  2. The possibility of eternal memory without forgetting in the digital age is a serious menace to human autonomy.

    “the possibility of “eternal memory” without forgetting at the digital age is a serious menace to human autonomy, to human essence.”

    Experiment, 2020. Lektorsky, Istoriya.

  3. People who use digital tools for information storage have lower memory retention and shallower information processing than those using traditional memory strategies.

    “Participants who used digital tools exhibited lower memory retention and shallower information processing compared to those employing traditional memory strategies.”

    Experiment, 2023. Musa et al., Khizanah al-Hikmah Jurnal Ilmu Perpustakaan Informasi dan Kearsipan.

  4. Data analytics algorithmically can manipulate electorates and entire democracies in new ways, while destabilising the free press.

    “Data analytics algorithmically can manipulate electorates and entire democracies in new ways, while destabilising the free press.”

    Experiment, 2018. Peters & Besley, Educational Philosophy and Theory.

  5. An educational intervention that supplements clinical practice results in improved knowledge in first-time hearing aid users.

    “An educational intervention that supplements clinical practice results in improved knowledge in first-time hearing aid users.”

    Randomized trial, 2015. Ferguson et al., American Journal of Audiology.

Digital tools change how you store and process information.

Using digital tools for storage results in lower memory retention and shallower processing. This creates a tension between reduced individual memory and increased attention to historical data.

Your autonomy faces a new kind of pressure.

The possibility of eternal memory without forgetting in the digital age is a serious menace to human autonomy. That may be why your past feels more present than your now.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

You sit in a dark room at night. You look at a picture from ten years ago. The memory of that day feels heavier than the room you are in now.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that digital environments create a tension between reduced individual memory retention and increased cascading flows of attention to historical data.

The sources do not provide a direct measurement of the degradation of shared historical narratives, only individual memory performance and attention flows.
