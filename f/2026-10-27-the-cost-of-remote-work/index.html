Does your home office protect your health?

File no. 0183October 27, 2026Technology

Social isolation is linked to coronary heart disease.

Individuals with poor social health are 30% more likely to develop coronary heart disease and stroke.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Social isolation is linked to stress, which leads to decreased perceived remote work productivity that, in turn, is related to remote work satisfaction.

    “the responses of 265 employees showed the deleterious role of social isolation in stress, which leads to decreased perceived remote work productivity that, in turn, is related to remote work satisfaction.”

    Observational study, 2020. Toscano & Zappalà, Sustainability. This shows a link, not a cause.

  2. Remote work can increase autonomy and enjoyment, but it also has substantial drawbacks, including the possibility of isolation and the absence of physical presence.

    “Telecommuting can increase autonomy and enjoyment, but it also has substantial drawbacks, including the possibility of isolation and the absence of physical presence.”

    Systematic review, 2024. Fatima et al., Bulletin of Business and Economics (BBE).

  3. The highest negative effect value was obtained for the association between burnout and the use of the digital environment (AI and RW) r = −0.478.

    “the highest negative effect value was obtained for the association between burnout and the use of the digital environment (AI and RW) r = −0.478.”

    Meta-analysis, 2025. Litan, Frontiers in Psychology.

  4. The highest positive value of the effect was obtained for the association between PWB and the use of the digital environment (AI, RW, and STT) r = 0.435.

    “The highest positive value of the effect was obtained for the association between PWB and the use of the digital environment (AI, RW, and STT) r = 0.435”

    Meta-analysis, 2025. Litan, Frontiers in Psychology.

  5. A systematic review concluded that individuals with poor social health (social isolation, lack of social support or loneliness) are 30% more likely to develop coronary heart disease (CHD) and stroke.

    “An international systematic review concluded that individuals with poor social health (social isolation, lack of social support or loneliness) are 30% more likely to develop coronary heart disease (CHD) and stroke.”

    Meta-analysis, 2022. Freak‐Poli et al., Health Promotion Journal of Australia.

Remote work shows mixed results.

Remote work can increase autonomy and enjoyment. However, it is also linked to social isolation, stress, and burnout. These factors are associated with decreased perceived productivity and satisfaction.

Autonomy carries a physical cost.

That may be why the convenience of working from home is linked to long-term health risks. The absence of physical presence in your workspace may influence your social health.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The evening routine.

You close your laptop at your kitchen table. The room is silent. You have finished your tasks, but you have not spoken to another person all day.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that remote work provides benefits like autonomy but is simultaneously linked to negative mental health outcomes and social isolation.

The study on social isolation and remote work satisfaction was based on an online survey of 265 employees and shows association rather than causation.
