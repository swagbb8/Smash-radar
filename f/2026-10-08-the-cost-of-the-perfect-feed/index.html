Whose body are you measuring yourself against?

File no. 0024October 8, 2026Social media

Manipulated photos are rated more positively than original ones.

Even when the reshaping of bodies is not noticed well, the edited image is preferred.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence backs this.

What the research found

  1. Manipulated photos were rated more positively than original photos, even though the reshaping of the bodies was not noticed very well.

    “Interestingly, the manipulated photos were rated more positively than the original photos. Although the use of filters and effects was detected, reshaping of the bodies was not noticed very well.”

    Experiment, 2016. Kleemans et al., Media Psychology.

  2. Exposure to manipulated Instagram photos directly leads to lower body image in girls aged 14–18.

    “Results showed that exposure to manipulated Instagram photos directly led to lower body image.”

    Experiment, 2016. Kleemans et al., Media Psychology.

  3. Girls with higher social comparison tendencies are negatively affected by exposure to manipulated photos.

    “Especially, girls with higher social comparison tendencies were negatively affected by exposure to the manipulated photos.”

    Experiment, 2016. Kleemans et al., Media Psychology.

  4. Body image was significantly more negative after viewing thin media images than after viewing images of either average size models, plus size models, or inanimate objects.

    “Body image was significantly more negative after viewing thin media images than after viewing images of either average size models, plus size models, or inanimate objects.”

    Meta-analysis, 2001. Groesz et al., International Journal of Eating Disorders.

  5. The negative effect of thin media images on body image was stronger for participants less than 19 years of age.

    “This effect was stronger for between-subjects designs, participants less than 19 years of age, and for participants who are vulnerable to activation of a thinness schema.”

    Meta-analysis, 2001. Groesz et al., International Journal of Eating Disorders.

  6. Internalization of body shape ideals is strongly linked to body dissatisfaction in younger individuals.

    “IBSI and body dissatisfaction were strongly linked: (a) in younger individuals (general attractiveness-ideal internalization);”

    Meta-analysis, 2021. Paterna et al., International Journal of Eating Disorders.

Social media exposure lowers body satisfaction.

Viewing manipulated Instagram photos directly leads to lower body image in girls aged 14–18. This effect is stronger for those with high social comparison tendencies.

Internalizing these ideals changes your self-perception.

Internalization of body shape ideals is strongly linked to body dissatisfaction in younger individuals. That may be why your self-image shifts after scrolling.

You lie in the dark and look at thin media images. Your reflection in the mirror feels different the next morning.

The honest summary

The evidence consistently shows that exposure to thin or manipulated beauty ideals on social media leads to lower body satisfaction, especially among adolescents.

While body-positive content shows promise in improving mood and satisfaction in the short term, its influence on social comparison remains limited.
