Dark traits favor opportunistic profit over promotion.

Research links dark triad traits to profit manipulation and bullying rather than direct promotion rates. The evidence on how these traits impact corporate structures is mixed.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. CSR has a positive impact on EM, indicating that CSR creates more favorable conditions for EM and supporting the opportunistic behavior hypothesis.

    “the results show that CSR has a positive impact on EM, indicating that CSR creates more favorable conditions for EM and supporting the opportunistic behavior hypothesis.”

    Experiment, 2024. Khanchel et al., European Management Review.

  2. Managers with moderate Machiavellian traits showed a higher tendency to maximize profit by manipulating results.

    “An opposite tendency was revealed for those with moderate Machiavellian traits.”

    Experiment, 2019. D’Souza et al., Revista Contabilidade & Finanças.

  3. Psychopathy is the strongest predictor of workplace bullying perpetration, followed by narcissism (r = 0.40 [95% CI 0.26–0.52]) and Machiavellianism (r = 0.35 [95% CI 0.13–0.53]).

    “Interpretation Machiavellianism, narcissism, and psychopathy are robust predictors of workplace bullying perpetration, with psychopathy most strongly implicated.”

    Meta-analysis, 2026. Sui et al., Frontiers in Psychology.

  4. CEO narcissism moderates the relationship between CSR and EM, influencing an increase in income.

    “Furthermore, CEO narcissism moderates the relationship between CSR and EM, influencing an increase in income.”

    Experiment, 2024. Khanchel et al., European Management Review.

  5. The combined effect of the three Dark Triad traits was significant and positive, revealing opportunistic profit maximization.

    “The combined effect of the three Dark Triad traits was significant and positive, revealing opportunistic profit maximization.”

    Experiment, 2019. D’Souza et al., Revista Contabilidade & Finanças.

  6. Firms pursuing internationalization may benefit from CEOs with a dark triad personality.

    “firms pursuing internationalization may benefit from CEOs with a dark triad personality.”

    Study, 2024. Nooshabadi et al., International Business Review.
