Does your phone actually make you feel closer?

File no. 0135October 21, 2026Human relationships

Digital tools can foster closeness and self-esteem.

Evidence suggests that digital communication can enhance self-esteem and foster closeness rather than simply reducing the rewards of face-to-face interaction. These findings are mixed or contested as there is no data on the neurological rewards of deep face-to-face interaction.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. Becoming self-aware by viewing one's own Facebook profile enhances self-esteem.

    “becoming self-aware by viewing one's own Facebook profile enhances self-esteem rather than diminishes it.”

    Experiment, 2011. Gonzales & Hancock, Cyberpsychology, behavior and social networking.

  2. Participants that updated their profiles and viewed their own profiles during the experiment also reported greater self-esteem.

    “Participants that updated their profiles and viewed their own profiles during the experiment also reported greater self-esteem”

    Experiment, 2011. Gonzales & Hancock, Cyberpsychology, behavior and social networking.

  3. The substitution of language for nonverbal cues in text-based computer-mediated communication systems transforms users’ reception, self-presentation, composition, and reciprocal reinforcement of messages in ways that create socially desirable relationships online.

    “the substitution of language for nonverbal cues, necessitated by text-based computer-mediated communication systems, transforms users’ reception, self-presentation, composition, and reciprocal reinforcement of messages in ways that create socially desirable relationships online.”

    Study, 2020. Walther & Whitty, Journal of Language and Social Psychology.

  4. Selective self-presentation in digital media is linked to intensified relationship formation.

    “selective self-presentation in digital media, which leads to intensified relationship formation, also influences impressions of the self.”

    Experiment, 2011. Gonzales & Hancock, Cyberpsychology, behavior and social networking.

  5. Video calling software allowed people to achieve greater closeness and connection with friends and family during the COVID-19 pandemic.

    “For many people, video calling software had become especially important, allowing them to achieve greater closeness and connection with their friends and family”

    Study, 2020. Watson et al., Media International Australia.

  6. Digital communication technologies had an increased presence in people’s domestic lives during lockdown.

    “Digital communication technologies had an increased presence in people’s domestic lives during lockdown.”

    Study, 2020. Watson et al., Media International Australia.

Technology changes how you present yourself.

Replacing nonverbal cues with text allows for selective self-presentation. This process is linked to intensified relationship formation and the creation of socially desirable relationships online.

Connection happens in new spaces.

That may be why you feel closer to people during periods of physical isolation. Digital tools allowed for greater connection with family and friends during the pandemic.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A face on a screen.

You sit in a dark room at night. You watch a friend's face on your phone. You feel less alone in the quiet.

The honest summary

The evidence suggests that digital communication can actually enhance self-esteem and foster closeness rather than simply reducing the rewards of face-to-face interaction.

The sources do not provide data on the neurological rewards of deep, focused face-to-face interaction or the specific impact of high-frequency low-effort messaging.
