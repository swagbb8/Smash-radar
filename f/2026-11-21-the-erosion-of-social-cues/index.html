Is your screen eroding your ability to read a room?

File no. 0314November 21, 2026Social media

Virtual environments can be used as therapeutic tools for social training.

Technology is being used to help people implement real-time social interaction.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Virtual environments shape moral reasoning, emotional regulation, and prosocial behavior.

    “It provides insights into how virtual environments shape moral reasoning, emotional regulation, and prosocial behavior.”

    Systematic review, 2025. Čekić, International Journal of Psychology.

  2. Because of the lack of non-verbal cues, computer-mediated communication messages could influence the understanding of emotions and attitudes, thus compromising meaningful communication and personal understanding of the other.

    “The premise of the author was that because of the lack of non-verbal cues, CMC messages could influence the understanding of emotions and attitudes, thus compromising meaningful communication and personal understanding of the other.”

    Study, 2019. Venter, HTS Teologiese Studies / Theological Studies. This shows a link, not a cause.

  3. Virtual characters and environments are a valuable assistive, educational and therapeutic tool for high-functioning autism to implement real-time social interaction.

    “Finally, we argue that VCs and environments are a valuable assistive, educational and therapeutic tool for HFA.”

    Review, 2014. Georgescu et al., Frontiers in Human Neuroscience.

  4. Digital communication is distinguished from face-to-face communication by a lack of non-verbal cues and time asynchrony.

    “unique characteristics of digital communication—such as lacking non-verbal cues or time asynchrony—clearly distinguish this new form of communication from traditional face-to-face communication.”

    Review, 2024. Strauss et al., Management Review Quarterly.

  5. Social Virtual Reality is a communication medium that includes non-verbal interaction and avatar communication.

    “The thematic synthesis reveals five primary domains shaping Social VR development: avatar communication, non-verbal interaction, virtual collaboration, ethical considerations, and inclusivity.”

    Systematic review, 2025. Renda Sandi & Kalfin, International Journal of Linguistics Communication and Broadcasting.

Digital communication lacks non-verbal cues.

Your digital world is shaping your social skills.

The honest summary

The evidence confirms that digital communication lacks real-time non-verbal cues which can hinder emotional understanding, but it also shows that technology is being developed to simulate or facilitate these interactions.

The sources provide a mix of literature reviews and theoretical premises rather than experimental data measuring the specific degradation of real-world intuition.
