The world you don’t see

Who is actually making the choice?

File no. 0015October 7, 2026Technology

Shopping sites use design to maximize profit.

Firms use proprietary A-B testing to confirm that dark patterns are profit maximizing. These design choices successfully manipulate consumers into making unintended purchases.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence backs this.

What the research found

  1. Older individuals tend to be more susceptible to dark patterns.

    “Older individuals tend to be more susceptible to dark patterns.”

    Experiment, 2023. Koh & Seah, Cleaner and Responsible Consumption.

  2. Decision architecture, not price, drove consumer purchasing decisions.

    “Decision architecture, not price, drove consumer purchasing decisions.”

    Experiment, 2020. Luguri & Strahilevitz, The Journal of Legal Analysis.

  3. In a study of 195 adult volunteers aged 19 to 53, participants were significantly more likely to select products with dark patterns.

    “Results indicated that participants were significantly more likely to select products with dark patterns.”

    Experiment, 2023. Koh & Seah, Cleaner and Responsible Consumption.

  4. The use of limited-time message dark pattern was significantly more effective than other dark patterns in inducing consumption.

    “The use of limited-time message dark pattern was significantly more effective than other dark patterns in inducing consumption.”

    Experiment, 2023. Koh & Seah, Cleaner and Responsible Consumption.

  5. Firms' proprietary A-B testing has revealed dark patterns to be profit maximizing.

    “Dark patterns are presumably proliferating because firms’ proprietary A-B testing has revealed them to be profit maximizing.”

    Experiment, 2020. Luguri & Strahilevitz, The Journal of Legal Analysis.

  6. Analyzing ~53K product pages from ~11K shopping websites, researchers discovered 1,818 dark pattern instances, representing 15 types and 7 broader categories.

    “Analyzing ~53K product pages from ~11K shopping websites, we discover 1,818 dark pattern instances, together representing 15 types and 7 broader categories.”

    Study, 2019. Mathur et al., Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction.

Design choice drives your spending.

Decision architecture, rather than the price of an item, drives your purchasing decisions. Researchers found that people are significantly more likely to select products with dark patterns.

Your choices are not yours alone.

That may be why you feel pushed toward a purchase. The systems you use are designed to influence your behavior through specific messages and layouts.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A countdown clock on a screen.

You see a timer ticking down on a product page. The message says the offer expires in minutes. You click the button to buy it now.

The honest summary

The evidence confirms that dark patterns are widespread and effective at influencing consumer behavior to maximize profit.

The study on awareness interventions found no clear evidence that video- or activity-based methods effectively reduce the impact of dark patterns.
