Did your house design decide your role?

File no. 0241November 6, 2026History

Ancient Egyptian households were not segregated by gender.

Archaeological evidence shows men and women shared domestic spaces and performed various tasks interchangeably.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. Women in Ancient Egypt performed a wide range of activities, including religious rituals and handicrafts, in the same spaces as their male counterparts.

    “Koltsida’s findings reveal that a wide range of activities, including religious rituals and handicrafts, were conducted by women in the same spaces as their male counterparts.”

    Study, 2024. Koltsida, Burning Farm.

  2. Archeological evidence suggests that household rooms in Ancient Egypt were not segregated along gender lines.

    “Morevover she questions the idea that household rooms were segregated along gender lines, suggesting that women and men may have simultaneously and interchangeably performed domestic chores and customs.”

    Study, 2024. Koltsida, Burning Farm.

  3. Smart home technologies can increase surveillance and control over domestic workers, potentially intensifying their workload and reinforcing traditional gender norms.

    “On the other hand, they can increase surveillance and control over domestic workers, potentially intensifying their workload and reinforcing traditional gender norms.”

    Systematic review, 2025. Sidik et al., Discourse.

  4. Smart home technologies can enhance workers' autonomy by reducing the physical demands of domestic tasks.

    “On the one hand, these technologies can enhance workers' autonomy by reducing the physical demands of domestic tasks.”

    Systematic review, 2025. Sidik et al., Discourse.

  5. The concept of the domestic realm is challenged by feminists because it has the potential to reinforce the subordination of women and confine them to traditional family roles.

    “Feminists persistently challenge, discuss, or reject the concept of the domestic realm due to its potential to reinforce the subordination of women and confine them to traditional family roles.”

    Study, 2023. Chee, Routledge eBooks.

The evidence on domestic spaces is mixed.

While some theories suggest architecture reinforces gender roles, historical evidence from Ancient Egypt shows shared spaces. Modern smart home tech may also reinforce or reduce domestic autonomy.
