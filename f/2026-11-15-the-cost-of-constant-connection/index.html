Your brain is being hijacked

Does your internet use affect your focus?

File no. 0287November 15, 2026Technology

Problematic internet use is linked to impaired executive functions.

Research shows a link between high levels of internet use and deficits in working memory and inhibition. This is not yet proven as a direct cause.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Brain structure changes in the temporal cortex and ventral striatum are linked to a loss of control over internet use.

    “Brain structure changes also occur in the temporal cortex and ventral striatum, compromising executive function in planning and reasoning and increasing impulsive risk, resulting in loss of control over internet use.”

    Study, 2019. Siste et al., Medical Journal of Indonesia.

  2. Problematic Usage of the Internet is linked to a neural pattern of increased activation in the left middle frontal gyrus and right superior parietal lobule during inhibitory control tasks.

    “We conclude that the observed increased activation in the left middle frontal gyrus and right superior parietal lobule during inhibitory control tasks is a neural pattern that is often associated with PUI.”

    Meta-analysis, 2026. Li et al., NeuroImage.

  3. Executive functioning problems are linked to academic course performance mediated by studying attention and a single classroom digital metacognition subscale.

    “Results indicated that executive functioning problems predicted academic course performance mediated by studying attention and a single classroom digital metacognition subscale concerning availability of strategies of when to use mobile phones during lectures.”

    Study, 2018. Rosen et al., Psicologí a Educativa.

  4. Problematic Internet Use is linked to impairment of executive functions, specifically working memory and inhibition.

    “the results indicated a good reliability (Cronbach’s α = 0. 810). Additionally, Structural Equation Modelling was performed as a confirmatory factor analysis, revealing that SPIUT scores correlated with executive functions impairment (working memory and inhibition)”

    Observational study, 2025. Baroni et al., Current Psychology.

  5. The use of technology-based interventions provides low-quality evidence for the rehabilitation of executive functions and memory for children and adolescents with TBI.

    “This review provides low-quality evidence for the use of technology-based interventions in the rehabilitation of executive functions and memory for children and adolescents with TBI.”

    Review, 2016. Linden et al., Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews.

Your brain patterns change with internet use.

Problematic internet use correlates with specific activation in the left middle frontal gyrus and right superior parietal lobule. These areas are involved in inhibitory control tasks.

Your control mechanisms may be weakening.

That may be why it feels harder to focus or stop scrolling. Changes in the temporal cortex and ventral striatum are linked to a loss of control over internet use.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The late night scroll.

You sit on your couch at 11 p.m. You intend to check one notification. You find yourself moving from link to link for an hour without a plan.

The honest summary

The sources show a correlation between problematic internet use and impaired executive functions and specific brain activity patterns, but they do not prove that high-speed internet causes these changes.

Several studies are observational or provide low-quality evidence, and the meta-analysis on brain activity notes that findings are fragmented and mixed.
