Does infinite scrolling actually damage your brain?

File no. 0181October 27, 2026The future

No evidence links infinite scrolling to neurological fatigue.

The research on dopamine rewards involves rats and does not measure human behavior or digital interfaces.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. Dopamine depletions in the nucleus accumbens increase the tendency for rats to take long pauses in responding of more than 20.0 s.

    “DA depletions also increased the tendency to take long pauses in responding (i. e. > 20.0 s), and this effect was evident across all schedules tested.”

    Study, 2005. Mingote et al., European Journal of Neuroscience.

  2. In rats, the drug SB-277011A blocks cocaine-enhanced brain stimulation reward and cocaine-seeking behavior.

    “In rats, acute administration of SB-277011A, a highly selective dopamine (DA) D(3) receptor antagonist, blocks cocaine-enhanced brain stimulation reward, cocaine-seeking behaviour and reinstatement of cocaine-seeking behaviour.”

    Study, 2005. Xi et al., European Journal of Neuroscience.

  3. SB-277011A failed to impede locomotor activity, impair rearing behaviour, produce catalepsy, or impair rotarod performance in rats.

    “SB-277011A (3, 12 or 24 mg/kg) failed to impede locomotor activity, failed to impair rearing behaviour, failed to produce catalepsy and failed to impair rotarod performance.”

    Study, 2005. Xi et al., European Journal of Neuroscience.

  4. Dopamine in the nucleus accumbens appears to be necessary for adapting to work-related response costs that provide a challenge to the organism.

    “Attachment of ratio requirements to interval schedules is a work-related response cost that provides a challenge to the organism, and DA in nucleus accumbens appears to be necessary for adapting to this challenge.”

    Study, 2005. Mingote et al., European Journal of Neuroscience.

  5. The drug SB-277011A reduces the cocaine dose-response break-point curve produced by a 24 mg/kg dose.

    “The reduction in the cocaine (0. 25-1. 0 mg/kg) dose-response break-point curve produced by 24 mg/kg SB-277011A is consistent with a reduction in cocaine's reinforcing efficacy.”

    Study, 2005. Xi et al., European Journal of Neuroscience.

  6. Infinite scrolling is a widely adopted feature that transforms how users interact with and consume content.

    “Infinite scrolling has emerged as a widely adopted feature, transforming the way users interact with and consume content.”

    Study, 2023. Sera, INTERANTIONAL JOURNAL OF SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH IN ENGINEERING AND MANAGEMENT.

The science of dopamine is incomplete.

Studies show dopamine in the nucleus accumbens helps rats adapt to work-related challenges. However, these biological mechanisms are not connected to the mechanics of infinite scrolling.

Your habits lack a biological blueprint.

The narrative of permanent neurological damage from scrolling is not supported by current data. That may be why the fear feels bigger than the evidence.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The honest summary

The sources provide data on dopamine receptors and rat behavior, but they do not connect these biological mechanisms to the specific mechanics of infinite scrolling.

The research on dopamine and rewards is conducted in rats and does not directly measure human behavior or digital interface usage.
