Does your gaming habit make you cold?

File no. 0207October 31, 2026The human brain

Video games do not desensitize your brain to pain.

Research shows no differences in brain responses when viewing emotional situations between excessive gamers and control subjects.,,

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. A study of 15 excessive users of violent games and control subjects showed no differences in brain responses when viewing pictures of emotional and neutral situations.

    “While the typical pattern of activations for empathy and theory of mind networks was seen, both groups showed no differences in brain responses.”

    Study, 2017. Szycik et al., Frontiers in Psychology.

  2. A longitudinal fMRI study on non-gamers found no evidence for desensitization in the empathy network for pain in the violent video game group at any time point.

    “We did not find any evidence for desensitization in the empathy network for pain in the violent video game group at any time point.”

    Experiment, 2018. Kühn et al., Neurosignals.

  3. The perception of others' pain were not significantly different in brain regions between groups of participants with and without long-time exposure to violent video games.

    “The results showed that the perception of others' pain were not significantly different in brain regions between groups, from which we could infer that the desensitization effect of VVGs was overrated.”

    Study, 2017. Gao et al., Frontiers in Psychology.

  4. Professional experience seems to desensitize physicians to the pain of others without necessarily helping them down-regulate their own personal distress.

    “Professional experience seems to desensitize physicians to the pain of others without necessarily helping them down-regulate their own personal distress.”

    Observational study, 2014. Gleichgerrcht & Jean Decety, Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience.

The evidence is mixed regarding how habits change empathy.

While professional experience links physicians to a desensitized perception of others' pain, research consistently fails to find neural desensitization from violent video games.

Media consumption is not rewriting your empathy.

That may be why you still feel the weight of human suffering despite the content you consume. The change happens through direct professional exposure.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

You sit in a dark room.

The screen glows. You watch a character fall. You still feel the phantom ache in your own chest.

The honest summary

The evidence contradicts the idea that violent media causes neural desensitization, though professional exposure to suffering is associated with a desensitized perception of pain.

The study on physicians is observational and shows an association rather than a proven cause for desensitization.
