Your brain is being hijacked

Why can you not put the phone down?

File no. 0186October 27, 2026Health and environment

Problematic smartphone use is linked to smaller brain structures.

Studies link social networking addiction and screen dependence to smaller areas of the striatum and amygdala.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Some studies show that people addicted to social networking sites and those showing screen dependence are linked to having smaller areas of the striatum and amygdala.

    “Some studies show that individuals addicted to social networking sites and those showing screen dependence, behaviors that often involve value-based decisions, are seen to have smaller areas of striatum and amygdala.”

    Study, 2020. Sovani, Annals of Indian Psychiatry.

  2. Yoga has been shown to have a significant improvement in overcoming addictive behavior and breaking the loop of reward systems pleasuring mechanism through its practices like meditation, pranayama, pratyahara, aasana, etc.

    “Yoga has been shown to have a significant improvement in overcoming addictive behavior and breaking the loop of reward systems pleasuring mechanism through its practices like meditation, pranayama, pratyahara, aasana, etc.”

    Review, 2026. Mishra et al., Naveen International Journal of Multidisciplinary Sciences (NIJMS).

  3. Impaired executive functioning and inhibitory control, which are typical to addiction, are linked to lower functional connectivity in fronto-striatal circuits.

    “Impaired executive functioning and inhibitory control, both of which are typical to addiction, are connected to lower functional connectivity in fronto-striatal circuits.”

    Study, 2020. Sovani, Annals of Indian Psychiatry.

  4. Neuroimaging studies showed convergent alterations in dopaminergic reward pathways and prefrontal-limbic circuitry involved in craving and impaired inhibitory control in adolescents and young adults.

    “Neuroimaging studies showed convergent alterations in dopaminergic reward pathways and prefrontal-limbic circuitry involved in craving and impaired inhibitory control.”

    Systematic review, 2026. Singh et al., Frontiers in psychology.

  5. Drug addiction involves allostatic changes in the brain reward and stress systems.

    “Drug addiction can be defined by a three-stage cycle - binge/intoxication, withdrawal/negative affect, and preoccupation/anticipation - that involves allostatic changes in the brain reward and stress systems.”

    Review, 2013. Koob, Frontiers in psychiatry.

  6. Problematic smartphone use is linked to mental health issues such as anxiety, depression, and sleep disturbances.

    “Problematic smartphone use (PSU) has emerged as a growing public health concern, with increasing evidence linking it to mental health issues such as anxiety, depression, and sleep disturbances.”

    Review, 2025. Ndayambaje & Okereke, Health Science Reports.

The mechanics of your reward system.

Addiction involves allostatic changes in the brain reward and stress systems. This is linked to lower functional connectivity in fronto-striatal circuits, affecting inhibitory control.

How this shapes your daily experience.

The evidence shows links between screen use and neurobiological changes in reward and stress circuits. That may be why you find it difficult to stop scrolling.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A common evening routine.

You sit on the couch in a dark room. Your thumb moves in a repetitive motion. The screen light reflects in your eyes as you wait for the next update.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that problematic smartphone use is linked to neurobiological changes in reward and stress circuits and smaller brain structures, but it does not explicitly confirm that notifications specifically cause the reshaping of dopamine pathways.

The sources provide observational links and general neurobiological descriptions of addiction rather than a direct causal study on smartphone notifications specifically.
