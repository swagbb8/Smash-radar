Are you being biologically redesigned for work?

File no. 0293November 16, 2026Money and power

There is no evidence for biological optimization.

Studies do not show workers are being biologically optimized for compliance. The evidence on this specific theory is non-existent.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. Digital workplace surveillance is linked to a 'surveillance tax' comprising software costs, management overhead, and high employee turnover.

    “this low-trust environment imposes a significant 'surveillance tax'-comprising software costs, management overhead, and high employee turnover-which creates a self-reinforcing 'insecurity loop'”

    Study, 2026. Majumdar, Journal of Human Resource Management.

  2. The need for education and training programs to suppress members' intention to violate information security policy should be emphasized.

    “it proves that the need of education and training programs suppressing members' neutralization intention to violate information security policy should be emphasized.”

    Observational study, 2014. Kim et al., The Scientific World JOURNAL.

  3. High-trust organisations that focus on outcomes rather than inputs are linked to superior revenue per employee, lower attrition rates, and enhanced organisational resilience.

    “By contrasting this model with high-trust organisations that focus on outcomes rather than inputs, the analysis quantifies the financial dividend of trust, evidenced by superior revenue per employee, lower attrition rates, and enhanced organisational resilience.”

    Study, 2026. Majumdar, Journal of Human Resource Management.

  4. Peter Pan Syndrome is linked to industrial psychology parameters such as productivity, belonging, leadership, teamwork and psychological endurance in business life.

    “This study examines the relationship between Industrial Pan Syndrome... and industrial psychology parameters such as productivity, belonging, leadership, teamwork and psychological endurance in business life.”

    “Compliance with MARS in the method section was generally inadequate in the random sample of 120 articles.”

    Meta-analysis, 2017. Schalken & Rietbergen, Frontiers in Psychology.

  6. Using computer vision-based surveillance in workplaces brings forth significant concerns.

    “With the growing capabilities of computer vision technologies, its application in monitoring workplaces brings forth significant concerns.”

    Study, 2025. Sebastian et al., AI and Ethics.

The mechanisms are psychological and operational.

Workplaces use digital surveillance and training programs to manage employee behavior. High-trust models focus on outcomes rather than monitoring inputs.

Compliance is managed through oversight.

That may be why you feel watched. It is not a change in your biology, but a cost of software and management overhead.
