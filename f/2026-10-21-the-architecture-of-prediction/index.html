Who owns the data from your routine?

File no. 0141October 21, 2026Social media

Predictive systems turn group data into individual predictions.

Statistical inferences about a group are transformed into specific predictions about you.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence backs this.

What the research found

  1. Predictive systems are linked to the transformation of statistical inferences about a group into individual predictions.

    “predictive systems transform statistical inferences, which provide knowledge about the cohort of training data donors, into individual predictions”

    Observational study, 2021. Mühlhoff, Ethics and Information Technology.

  2. Convenience is linked to an ideology that is central to Amazon's abilities to exploit the behavioral surplus of its customers.

    “convenience operates not just as a coveted consumer product but also as an ideology that is central to Amazon's abilities to exploit the behavioral surplus of its customers”

    Study, 2021. Huberman, Economic Anthropology.

  3. Agribusinesses are linked to buying big data for its predictive powers while bypassing farmers.

    “Agribusinesses are buying into big data for its predictive powers, bypassing farmers and aggregating previously proprietary farming data.”

    Study, 2016. Carbonell, Internet Policy Review.

  4. In 2016, 89 percent of the revenues of Alphabet's parent company came from Google's targeted advertising programs.

    “In 2016, 89 percent of the revenues of its parent company, Alphabet, derived from Google’s targeted advertising programs.”

    Observational study, 2023. Zuboff, Routledge eBooks.

  5. Google processes over 40,000 search queries every second on average, which is linked to its domination of the internet.

    “The scale of raw-material flows is reflected in Google’s domination of the internet, processing over 40,000 search queries every second on average”

    Observational study, 2023. Zuboff, Routledge eBooks.

Convenience masks the extraction of your behavior.

The ideology of convenience allows companies to exploit your behavioral surplus. They use this data to create systems of certainty and extraction.

Your habits are becoming a form of capital.

That may be why your daily choices feel like personal decisions. They might be the raw material for new forms of capital accumulation.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A simple search for a new product.

You type a few words into a search bar to find a gift. The system records the query to refine its model of your life.

The honest summary

The sources show that large corporations use behavioral surplus and predictive analytics to create systems of certainty and extraction across various industries.

The evidence regarding the link between behavioral surplus and specific outcomes is primarily observational and does not establish direct causation.
