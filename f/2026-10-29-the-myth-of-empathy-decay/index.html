Does online behavior change your brain?

File no. 0196October 29, 2026Social media

There is no evidence for neurological empathy degradation.

The data does not show that online incivility diminishes your neurological capacity for empathy.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. Mindfulness reduces the negative impact of toxic leadership by making conducive environments less conducive and susceptible followers less susceptible.

    “The results indicated mindfulness did reduce the negative impact: conducive environments were less conducive and susceptible followers were less susceptible.”

    Study, 2018. Cheng, Pepperdine Digital Commons (Pepperdine University).

  2. The offline cultural environment in which players live is linked to toxic behavior tendencies in multiplayer online games.

    “Our findings underscore the role that the offline cultural environments play in TB in MOBAs”

    Observational study, 2023. Kordyaka et al., ACM Transactions on Social Computing.

  3. Toxic workplace environment mediates the relationship between despotic leadership and employee turnover intention.

    “Toxic workplace environment mediates the relationship between despotic leadership and employee turnover intention.”

    Observational study, 2022. Iqbal et al., Behavioral Sciences.

  4. Cognitive distraction mediates the relationship between despotic leadership and employee turnover intention.

    “Similarly, cognitive distraction mediates the relationship between despotic leadership and employee turnover intention.”

    Observational study, 2022. Iqbal et al., Behavioral Sciences.

  5. Toxic workplace environment is linked to employee turnover intention.

    “Toxic workplace environment correlates with employee turnover intention.”

    Observational study, 2022. Iqbal et al., Behavioral Sciences.

  6. Cognitive distraction is linked to employee turnover intention.

    “Cognitive distraction also correlates with employee turnover intention.”

    Observational study, 2022. Iqbal et al., Behavioral Sciences.

Toxic environments drive turnover.

Toxic workplaces and despotic leadership are linked to employee turnover intentions. Cognitive distraction also mediates this relationship.

How you process your surroundings matters.

That may be why mindfulness is used to make followers less susceptible to toxic leadership. It changes how you interact with your environment.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The honest summary

The sources show that toxic environments correlate with workplace turnover and that mindfulness can mitigate the effects of toxic leadership, but they do not address neurological empathy degradation.

None of the provided sources contain data regarding neurological capacity or the specific effect of online incivility on empathy.
