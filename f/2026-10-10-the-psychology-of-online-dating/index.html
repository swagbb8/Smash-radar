Online dating has specific psychological risks.

The evidence shows that online dating is linked to negative impacts on body image and mental health.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. A significant positive relationship between the amount of online communication and perceptions of relational messages (intimacy, composure, informality, social orientation), forecasts of the future of the relationship, and information seeking behavior when meeting their partner FtF was found to be curvilinear.

    “Results were consistent with the hypothesized curvilinear relationship between the amount of online communication and perceptions of relational messages (intimacy, composure, informality, social orientation), forecasts of the future of the relationship, and information seeking behavior when meeting their partner FtF.”

    Study, 2014. Ramirez et al., Journal of Computer-Mediated Communication.

  2. Over 85% (n = 19) of studies reported a significant negative impact of dating app use on body image.

    “Over 85% (n = 19) of studies reported a significant negative impact of dating app use on body image, and 48. 6% (n = 14) of studies reported significant negative impacts on mental health and wellbeing.”

    Systematic review, 2024. Bowman et al., Computers in Human Behavior.

  3. A significant positive relationship between playing MMOs and social well-being was concluded, irrespective of one's age and/or their casual or immersed gaming patterns.

    “A significant positive relationship between playing MMOs and social well-being was concluded, irrespective of one's age and/or their casual or immersed gaming patterns.”

    Systematic review, 2021. Raith et al., Frontiers in Psychology.

  4. Men who discussed their mate preferences tended to report a higher likelihood of a second date than other participants.

    “men were generally more direct than women (e.g. , by sending the first message), and those who discussed their mate preferences tended to report a higher likelihood of a second date than other participants.”

    Study, 2019. Sharabi & Dykstra-DeVette, Journal of Social and Personal Relationships.

  5. Physical attractiveness is the key criteria for men, while women, being much more demanding, prioritize socio-economic attributes when choosing a male partner.

    “Physical attractiveness is the key criteria for men, while women, being much more demanding, prioritize socio-economic attributes when choosing a male partner.”

    Review, 2016. Abramova et al., Proceedings of the ... Annual Hawaii International Conference on System Sciences/Proceedings of the Annual Hawaii International Conference on System Sciences.

Communication patterns shape the outcome.

Relationship success depends on specific communication strategies. The amount of online interaction before meeting a partner in person affects perceptions of intimacy and future forecasts.

The medium changes the dynamic.

That may be why your online interactions feel different from physical ones. The way you talk online creates a specific framework for how you see your partner's personality and future.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that online dating has specific psychological risks and that relationship outcomes are influenced by communication patterns rather than being simply similar to offline dating.

The sources do not provide a direct comparison of overall relationship success rates between online and offline couples.
