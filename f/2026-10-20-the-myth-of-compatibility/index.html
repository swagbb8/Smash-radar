Do you need to be similar to be happy?

File no. 0127October 20, 2026Human relationships

Similarity is not a universal predictor of satisfaction.

Some studies show personality similarity is unrelated to satisfaction, while others find it is positively associated.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. Similarity in personality traits was unrelated to relationship satisfaction.

    “Similarity in personality traits was unrelated to relationship satisfaction.”

    Study, 2017. Leikas et al., Personality and Individual Differences.

  2. The more satisfied individuals were with their friendships, the more similar they perceived their friends to be to themselves.

    “Consistent with the attraction-similarity hypothesis, the more satisfied individuals were with their friendships the more similar they perceived their friends to be to themselves.”

    Experiment, 2005. Morry, Journal of Social and Personal Relationships.

  3. Women’s relationship satisfaction was related to similarity in left-right and liberal-conservative political attitudes.

    “Women’s relationship satisfaction was related to similarity in left-right and liberal-conservative political attitudes”

    Study, 2017. Leikas et al., Personality and Individual Differences.

  4. Actual similarity was positively associated with female relationship satisfaction, controlling for personality traits of both partners.

    “Actual similarity was positively associated with female relationship satisfaction, controlling for personality traits of both partners.”

    Study, 2011. Decuyper et al., Personal Relationships.

  5. Couples high in Conscientiousness are more satisfied with their marital life.

    “Couples high in Conscientiousness are more satisfied with their marital life.”

    Meta-analysis, 2020. Sayehmiri et al., BMC Psychology.

  6. Couples high in Neuroticism experience lower levels of marital satisfaction.

    “Couples high in Neuroticism experience lower levels of marital satisfaction”

    Meta-analysis, 2020. Sayehmiri et al., BMC Psychology.

Satisfaction may create the perception of similarity.

Research shows that people who are more satisfied with their friendships perceive those friends as more similar to themselves. The evidence is mixed and depends on what type of similarity is measured.

You might be looking for the wrong signal.

That may be why you feel a connection even when your personalities differ. You might be perceiving common ground because you enjoy the bond, not because it exists.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A quiet dinner at a kitchen table.

The honest summary

The evidence is inconsistent, showing that similarity is not a universal predictor of satisfaction and may instead be a byproduct of satisfaction.

The findings vary significantly depending on whether the study measures actual similarity, perceived similarity, or specific types of similarity like political attitudes.

Is the bond real, or just a reflection of your own happiness?
