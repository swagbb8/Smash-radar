Is your personality being rewritten by an algorithm?

File no. 0153October 23, 2026Technology

The evidence does not show algorithms reshape your personality.

While algorithms can reproduce biases from training data, there is no current evidence linking them to the active reshaping of long-term personality traits.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. In a population of patients with common mental disorders, personality status showed many changes over time, inconsistent with the view that personality disorder is a persistent or stable condition.

    “In this population of patients with common mental disorders, personality status showed many changes over time, inconsistent with the view that personality disorder is a persistent or stable condition.”

    Randomized trial, 2021. Yang et al., Australian & New Zealand Journal of Psychiatry.

  2. The increase in diagnoses within the Cluster A and C groups suggests personality disorder generally increases in frequency as people age.

    “The increase in diagnoses within the Cluster A and C groups suggests personality disorder generally increases in frequency as people age.”

    Randomized trial, 2021. Yang et al., Australian & New Zealand Journal of Psychiatry.

  3. For the drivers of extroverted personality, the fluctuation of personality states in the high-risk scenario was more pronounced.

    “Furthermore, for the drivers of extroverted personality, the fluctuation of personality states in the high-risk scenario was more pronounced.”

    Experiment, 2020. Wang et al., International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health.

  4. Choice architecture interventions that target the organization and structure of choice alternatives consistently outperform interventions that focus on the description of alternatives or the reinforcement of behavioral intentions.

    “Across behavioral domains, interventions that target the organization and structure of choice alternatives (decision structure) consistently outperform interventions that focus on the description of alternatives (decision information) or the reinforcement of behavioral intentions (decision assistance).”

    Meta-analysis, 2022. Mertens et al., Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America.

  5. Participants’ behavior was sensitive to deceleration nudges but not acceleration nudges.

    “We found that participants’ behavior was sensitive to deceleration nudges but not acceleration nudges.”

    Experiment, 2024. Zgonnikov et al., International Journal of Human-Computer Studies.

  6. Algorithms can create or reproduce distortions and inaccuracies unintentionally embedded in the training data, which causes the manifestation of algorithmic bias.

    “Algorithms can create or reproduce distortions and inaccuracies unintentionally embedded in the training data, which causes the manifestation of algorithmic bias.”

    Study, 2023. Васильевич & Denisov, Theory and Practice of Forensic Science. This shows a link, not a cause.

Personality is not a fixed state.

Research shows personality status changes over time and can fluctuate based on external situations. Personality disorders are not necessarily persistent conditions.

Your environment shapes your choices.

Choice architecture can nudge your behavior by changing how options are structured. This suggests your environment influences your actions more than your traits are being rewritten.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that personality is dynamic and can be influenced by choice architecture and external situations, but it does not link these specific mechanisms to the active reshaping of personality by social media algorithms.

None of the provided sources specifically study social media algorithms or their long-term effects on personality traits.
