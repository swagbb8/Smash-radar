Your gut feeling connects rewards to actions.

The anterior cingulate cortex is involved in emotion because it links rewards to actions.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. The ventral-rostral portions of the ACC and mPFC have a regulatory role with respect to limbic regions involved in generating emotional responses.

    “ventral-rostral portions of the ACC and mPFC have a regulatory role with respect to limbic regions involved in generating emotional responses.”

    Review, 2011. Etkin et al., Trends in cognitive sciences.

  2. The anterior cingulate cortex is involved in emotion because it connects rewards to actions.

    “because the anterior cingulate cortex connects rewards to actions, it is involved in emotion”

    Review, 2019. Rolls, Brain Structure and Function.

  3. Negative emotional stimuli activate a broad network of brain regions, including the medial prefrontal (mPFC) and anterior cingulate (ACC) cortices.

    “Negative emotional stimuli activate a broad network of brain regions, including the medial prefrontal (mPFC) and anterior cingulate (ACC) cortices.”

    Review, 2011. Etkin et al., Trends in cognitive sciences.

  4. The prefrontal cortex is considered to be the substrate of highest cognitive functions.

    “the prefrontal cortex (PFC) is considered to be the substrate of highest cognitive functions.”

    Review, 2021. Kolk & RAKIC, Neuropsychopharmacology.

  5. The expansion of cortical regions in humans relative to nonhuman primates may explain uniquely human capacities to plan, solve problems, and create cultural and technological artifacts.

    “The expansion of these regions in the human relative to the nonhuman primate brain may explain uniquely human capacities to use language productively, plan, solve problems, and create cultural and technological artifacts”

    Meta-analysis, 2009. Binder et al., Cerebral cortex (New York, N.Y. : 1991).

The prefrontal cortex manages high-level cognition.

The prefrontal cortex is the substrate of highest cognitive functions. It regulates emotional responses from limbic regions.

Logic and emotion occupy different spaces.

That may be why your immediate feelings often feel distinct from your deliberate plans. Your brain processes reward and emotion in specific regions separate from your highest cognitive functions.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The split second before you speak.

You stand in a quiet room. You feel a sudden urge to say something. Your body reacts before your mind can find the words to explain why.

The honest summary

The sources show that the prefrontal cortex handles high-level cognition and regulation, while the cingulate cortex and limbic regions are involved in emotional processing and reward.

The sources do not provide evidence that the amygdala specifically triggers physiological responses that precede conscious thought or hijack rational decision-making.
