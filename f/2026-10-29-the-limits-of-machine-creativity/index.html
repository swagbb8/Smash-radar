The honest summary

The evidence shows that generative AI acts as a catalyst for human creativity and impacts digital domains, but it currently struggles with sustained novelty.

The studies focus on human-AI interaction and specific educational contexts rather than the underlying computational linguistics of predicting statistical probabilities of novelty.
