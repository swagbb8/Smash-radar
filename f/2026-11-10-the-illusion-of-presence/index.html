Are you actually working while you are at your desk?

File no. 0259November 10, 2026Psychology

Presenteeism does not always directly link to productivity loss.

While presenteeism is linked to productivity loss through anxiety states or increased headache severity, it does not always have a direct relationship with health-related loss of productivity.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Tension-Type Headache presenteeism does not directly relate to health-related loss of productivity due to physical or mental health problems.

    “The results showed that TTH presenteeism did not directly relate to health-related loss of productivity, either due to physical, or mental health problems.”

    Randomized trial, 2018. Monzani et al., PLoS ONE.

  2. Tension-Type Headache presenteeism decreases patients' productivity as a consequence of reduced physical and mental health through an anxiety-state.

    “However, through anxiety-state, TTH presenteeism decreased patients' productivity, as consequence of reduced physical and mental health.”

    Randomized trial, 2018. Monzani et al., PLoS ONE.

  3. Tension-Type Headache presenteeism indirectly decreases patients' productivity as consequence of reduced physical health by increasing the severity of the headache.

    “Moreover, by increasing the severity of the Tension-Type Headache, TTH presenteeism indirectly decreased patients' productivity as consequence of reduced physical health (but not mental health).”

    Randomized trial, 2018. Monzani et al., PLoS ONE.

  4. Only cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia improved both sleep problems and presenteeism compared with a control group.

    “Only CBT-I improved both sleep problems and presenteeism compared with a control group.”

    Systematic review, 2021. Takano et al., BioPsychoSocial Medicine.

  5. Presenteeism in agricultural workers is linked to a poor work safety climate, female workers, workers dissatisfied with management, and sunscreen not available at the workplace.

    “Poor work safety climate, female workers, workers dissatisfied with management, and sunscreen not available at the workplace were strongly associated with the prevalence of presenteeism.”

    Systematic review, 2023. Siqueira et al., Workplace Health & Safety.

  6. The common health conditions most interfering with work were neck pain or stiff shoulders, low back pain, and mental illnesses.

    “The common health conditions most interfering with work were neck pain or stiff shoulders, low back pain, and mental illnesses.”

    Observational study, 2020. Yoshimoto et al., Journal of Occupational and Environmental Medicine.

The mechanisms of lost productivity are specific.

Presenteeism decreases productivity as a consequence of reduced physical and mental health through an anxiety-state. It can also reduce productivity by increasing the severity of headaches. The evidence for these links is mixed and contested.

Your presence is not your performance.

That may be why you feel exhausted despite showing up. Your productivity loss might be driven by specific psychological states like anxiety rather than just being at your desk.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The office chair at 3 p.m.

You sit at your desk with a dull ache in your head. You check your emails while your focus fades. You are physically there, but your output is slowing down as your anxiety rises.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that presenteeism is a complex phenomenon where the link to lost productivity often depends on specific health conditions and psychological states.

There is significant uncertainty and heterogeneity regarding the measurement of presenteeism across different studies.
