Did technology choose your life?

File no. 0145October 22, 2026History

Technology is not an independent force.

The evidence shows a complex tension between technological determinism and the active roles of human leadership and societal needs.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Technological lock-in is linked to the ability of a limited set of actors to force subsequent generations onto a certain technological trajectory.

    “Technological lock-in refers to the ability of a limited set of actors to force subsequent generations onto a certain technological trajectory”

    Study, 2021. Héder, Információs Társadalom.

  2. New media respond to the needs of society by including citizens as collaborators in production processes.

    “the new media respond to the needs of society by incorporating the citizen as a reason for its purpose and as a collaborator in production processes.”

    Study, 2020. Orosa et al., Media and Communication.

  3. School technology leaders sometimes adopt technology for its own sake.

    “School technology leaders placed priority on embracing technological change, sometimes adopting technology for its own sake.”

    Study, 2017. Webster, Symposion.

  4. Technological determinism can be a result of design being a balance between what society wants and what is technically possible.

    “technological determinism is not necessarily a “bad” thing, but a natural result of design being a balance between what is societally desirable and technically possible.”

    Study, 2020. Hällström, International Journal of Technology and Design Education.

  5. Journalists often believe that technology has great power and independent agency.

    “several studies show that journalists ascribe great power and independent agency to technology.”

    Study, 2010. Örnebring, Journalism.

  6. Technology leadership is linked to problem-solving, facilitation, human orientation, interpersonal skills, continuous innovation, organizational support, personal attributes, strategic foresight, and ethical practices.

    “emphasizing problem-solving, facilitation, human orientation, interpersonal skills, continuous innovation, organizational support, personal attributes, strategic foresight, and ethical practices.”

    Systematic review, 2024. Gananta et al., Dinasti International Journal of Education Management And Social Science.

The systems you use are balanced.

Design is a balance between what society wants and what is technically possible. A limited set of actors can force a technological trajectory on future generations.

Leadership shapes your digital landscape.

That may be why your tools feel inevitable. They are often the result of specific human attributes like strategic foresight and organizational support rather than just progress.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A school computer lab.

You sit in a row of desks. The software you use was chosen by a leader who may have adopted it for its own sake rather than your specific needs.

The honest summary

The evidence shows a complex tension between technological determinism as a historical driver and the active roles of human leadership, societal needs, and specific actor-driven trajectories.

The sources provide various theoretical perspectives and specific industry examples (journalism, education) rather than a comprehensive historical analysis of industrial history.
