The uncomfortable truth

Is your immune system being suppressed?

File no. 0284November 15, 2026Science and reality

Chronic inflammation and immunosuppression are linked to increased mortality.

Longer exposure to immunosuppression is associated with higher mortality in patients with necrotizing soft-tissue infections. This evidence is linked to specific pathological outcomes and does not directly analyze pharmaceutical interventions.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. In patients with inflammatory bowel disease, longer duration of chronic inflammation and longer exposure to immunosuppression is linked to an increasing risk of cancer development.

    “As the population of patients with IBD grows older, with longer duration of chronic inflammation and longer exposure to immunosuppression, there is an increasing risk of cancer development.”

    Review, 2016. Axelrad et al., World Journal of Gastroenterology.

  2. Silencing IRF1 and LXRβ suppressed microglial phagocytic activity for polystyrene microspheres as well as fAβ42.

    “IRF1 and LXRβ silencing suppressed microglial phagocytic activity for polystyrene microspheres as well as fAβ42”

    Experiment, 2019. Gao et al., Glia.

  3. Chronic inflammation can create an environment that allows for tumor formation and progression.

    “One potential mechanism is that chronic inflammation can generate an immunosuppressive microenvironment that allows advantages for tumor formation and progression.”

    Review, 2015. Wang & DuBois, Carcinogenesis.

  4. In macrophages, pro-inflammatory stimuli induce a metabolic switch from oxidative phosphorylation to glycolysis.

    “In macrophages, pro-inflammatory stimuli induce a metabolic switch from oxidative phosphorylation to glycolysis, a phenomenon similar to the Warburg effect well characterized in tumor cells.”

    Review, 2020. Lauro & Limatola, Frontiers in Immunology.

  5. Immunosuppression is linked to a moderate or high certainty of association with increased mortality among patients with necrotizing soft-tissue infections.

    “Patient factors with a moderate or high certainty of association with increased mortality include older age, chronic liver disease, chronic kidney disease, high Charlson Comorbidity Index, and immunosuppression.”

    Meta-analysis, 2026. Kruger et al., Critical care medicine.

The environment of your body changes.

Chronic inflammation creates an environment that allows for tumor formation. In your macrophages, pro-inflammatory stimuli cause a metabolic switch from oxidative phosphorylation to glycolysis.

Your internal balance is shifting.

That may be why persistent inflammation or suppressed immune responses can lead to long-term health risks. Your body's metabolic processes change in response to these states.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that chronic inflammation and immunosuppression are associated with serious health risks like cancer and increased mortality, but it does not explicitly state that modern medicine is systematically dampening a natural healing response.

The sources focus on the pathological consequences of inflammation and immunosuppression rather than providing a direct analysis of modern pharmaceutical interventions on natural homeostasis.
