The dark side of modern life

Is empathy a tool for kindness or a weapon for control?

File no. 0180October 26, 2026Human relationships

High empathy can facilitate undesirable behaviors.

Individuals with high cognitive empathy can use these skills to manipulate others in social and workplace settings. This finding is contested as evidence distinguishes between cognitive and affective empathy.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Good emotional intelligence skills can facilitate undesirable workplace behaviors.

    “This study is the first to demonstrate that good emotional intelligence skills can facilitate undesirable workplace behaviours.”

    Experiment, 2020. Hyde et al., Australian Journal of Psychology.

  2. Children involved in indirect aggression also report high cognitive empathy and lower pro-social behavior.

    “Mostly children involved in indirect aggression also report high cognitive empathy and have lower pro-social behavior that suggest that some bullies use this cognitive ability to engage in successful acts of manipulation in social groups”

    Experiment, 2016. Sehar & Fatima, Journal of behavioural sciences.

  3. Machiavellianism is linked to significant affective empathy deficits.

    “This review underscores differential empathy profiles across Dark Triad traits, with significant affective empathy deficits in Psychopathy and Machiavellianism”

    Systematic review, 2025. Shukla & Upadhyay, Frontiers in psychiatry.

  4. Narcissists are the most likely of the three Dark Triad dimensions to understand others' states of mind to use that knowledge to their advantage.

    “narcissists would be, among these three dimensions, those most likely to understand others' states of mind and thus be able to use this knowledge to their advantage”

    Review, 2023. Duradoni et al., European Journal of Investigation in Health Psychology and Education.

  5. In males, both forms of emotional manipulation most strongly correlated with Machiavellianism, followed by narcissism, then psychopathy.

    “In males, both forms of emotional manipulation most strongly correlated with Machiavellianism, followed by narcissism, then psychopathy.”

    Experiment, 2020. Hyde et al., Australian Journal of Psychology.

  6. Narcissism is linked to a negative association with affective empathy (r= -.134, p 2 range: 40.56% - 94.03%).

    “Meta-analysis showed Narcissism was negatively associated with affective empathy (r= -. 134, p 2 range: 40. 56% - 94. 03%).”

    Systematic review, 2025. Shukla & Upadhyay, Frontiers in psychiatry.

Understanding is not the same as feeling.

Narcissists are highly likely to understand others' states of mind to use that knowledge to their advantage. These individuals often have significant deficits in affective empathy, which is the ability to feel for others.

The danger of a skilled mind.

That may be why you find yourself trusting people who seem to understand you perfectly. Their ability to mirror your emotions may be a tool for manipulation rather than a sign of kindness.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The office meeting.

You sit in a glass room. A colleague listens to your concerns with intense focus. They mirror your tone and use your own words to steer the project in a direction that benefits only them. You feel understood, yet you feel used later that evening alone at your desk.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that high empathy (specifically cognitive empathy) is linked to manipulation and deceptive behaviors in individuals with Dark Triad traits.

The sources distinguish between cognitive empathy (understanding others) and affective empathy (feeling for others), showing that manipulation is often linked to the former while the latter is often deficient.
