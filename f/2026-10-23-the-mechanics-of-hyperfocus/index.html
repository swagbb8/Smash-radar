Is your deep focus actually a malfunction?

File no. 0151October 23, 2026The human brain

Hyperfocus is linked to addictive behaviors.

Evidence connects hyperfocus to malfunctions in attentional control, specifically in people with ADHD. This link is observed in both addictive behaviors and exercise addiction.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Neurotransmitters make tumor cells more likely to move and spread by changing gene expression.

    “Neurotransmitters thus induce a metastatogenic tumor cell type by directly regulating gene expression and increased migratory activity”

    Study, 2004. Lang et al., International Journal of Cancer.

  2. The strength of serotonin 5-hydroxytryptamine receptor subtype 2a co-localization is linked to lower awareness scores and lower social scores in people with ASD.

    “the awareness score in the Social Responsiveness Scale (ρ = -0. 475, p = 0. 009) and the social score in the Autism Diagnostic Observation Schedule (ρ = -0. 415, p = 0. 049) exhibited negative correlations with the strength of ReHo co-localization of serotonin 5-hydroxytryptamine receptor subtype 2a.”

    Study, 2025. Shi et al., CNS Neuroscience & Therapeutics.

  3. Hyperfocus is linked to the Inattention score at a significantly higher rate than it is linked to the Hyperactive score.

    “The correlation between HFS and the Inattention Score was significantly higher than that between HFS and the Hyperactive Score (p < 0. 001).”

    Observational study, 2023. Ishii et al., Frontiers in Psychiatry.

  4. Hyperfocus may be linked to addictive behavior in people with ADHD as a result of a malfunction in attentional control.

    “Our findings suggest that hyperfocus may play an important role in addictive behavior in ADHD as a manifestation of attentional control malfunction.”

    Observational study, 2023. Ishii et al., Frontiers in Psychiatry.

  5. Exercise frequency is linked to the total score of the exercise addiction scale and its sub-dimensions of extreme focus and emotion change.

    “It was determined that there was a statistically significant difference between the exercise frequency variable and the total score of the exercise addiction scale and the sub-dimensions of the scale, extreme focus and emotion change, tolerance development and passion total score (p < 0. 05).”

    Study, 2022. Erdoğan & Bozkurt, Journal of Educational Issues.

  6. Hyperfocus symptoms are linked to higher Inattention and Hyperactive scores in people with ADHD traits.

    “Results ADHD traits were associated with higher IAT scores (p < 0. 001) and higher HFS scores (p < 0. 001).”

    Observational study, 2023. Ishii et al., Frontiers in Psychiatry.

The evidence is mixed.

Research shows hyperfocus is associated with ADHD traits and exercise addiction. However, there is no evidence that this is an evolutionary survival mechanism or a specific neurotransmitter-driven flow state.

That may be why you lose time.

Your ability to focus deeply might not be a skill you mastered. It may be a result of how your brain manages attention, sometimes leading to behaviors that feel like addiction.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that hyperfocus is associated with ADHD traits, addictive behaviors, and exercise addiction, but it does not establish a causal link to evolutionary survival or specific neurotransmitter-driven 'flow' states.

The sources primarily show associations rather than causation, and none of the provided texts specifically define or analyze the 'flow state' as an evolutionary mechanism.
