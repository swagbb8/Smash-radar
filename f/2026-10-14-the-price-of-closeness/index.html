Who are you spending your hours on?

File no. 0082October 14, 2026Human relationships

Singles invest more in friendships for self-esteem.

Greater friendship investment predicts higher friendship quality and self-esteem later on. This association is stronger for single people than for those in partnerships.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence backs this.

What the research found

  1. Within-person fluctuations in friendship quality predicted within-person fluctuations in self-esteem, and this association was stronger for singles than for partnered people.

    “Multilevel modeling revealed that within-person fluctuations in friendship quality predicted within-person fluctuations in self-esteem, and this association was stronger for singles than for partnered people.”

    Observational study, 2021. Fisher et al., Social Psychological and Personality Science.

  2. Singles invested more in their friendships than partnered people, and greater friendship investment predicted greater friendship quality and self-esteem later on.

    “A cross-sectional mediation analysis also revealed that singles invested more in their friendships than partnered people, and greater friendship investment predicted greater friendship quality and self-esteem later on.”

    Observational study, 2021. Fisher et al., Social Psychological and Personality Science.

  3. Two types of everyday talk predicted changes in closeness.

    “Two types of everyday talk predicted changes in closeness.”

    Study, 2018. Hall, Journal of Social and Personal Relationships.

  4. The number of interworldview friendships in the first year on campus is positively associated with pluralism orientation.

    “Results revealed that, holding constant other predictors, the number of interworldview friendships in the first year on campus is positively associated with pluralism orientation.”

    Observational study, 2021. Hudson et al., Teachers College Record The Voice of Scholarship in Education.

  5. Hours together was associated with closer friendships.

    “Hours together was associated with closer friendships.”

    Study, 2018. Hall, Journal of Social and Personal Relationships.

  6. Time spent engaging in leisure activities also predicted closeness.

    “Time spent engaging in leisure activities also predicted closeness.”

    Study, 2018. Hall, Journal of Social and Personal Relationships.

Closeness is a product of shared time.

Hours spent together and engagement in leisure activities are associated with closer friendships. Two types of everyday talk also predict changes in closeness.

Time is the currency of connection.

That may be why your sense of self-worth is tied to the hours you spend with others. Your social circle may be a reflection of your time budget.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The coffee shop table.

You sit across from a friend for two hours. You do not talk about big plans. You talk about the weather and your day. This is the work of closeness.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that time spent together and engagement in activities are associated with closer friendships and higher self-esteem, particularly for single individuals.

The studies primarily show associations rather than proving that spending time together is the direct cause of friendship closeness.
