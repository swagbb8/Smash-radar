Does your search for the perfect partner create the conflict you want to avoid?

File no. 0235November 5, 2026Human relationships

The belief that a partner must meet all your needs is linked to higher conflict.

While you seek depth, this specific expectation correlates with friction in the relationship. This finding is based on observational studies and does not establish a causal link.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. The belief that partners must meet all needs of each other is linked to the conflict subscale of relationship quality.

    ““We must meet all needs of each other” significantly predicted the conflict subscale”

    Study, 2021. Dost & Aras, Pegem Journal of Education and Instruction.

  2. Romantic idealism is linked to the depth subscale of relationship quality.

    ““We must be able to change each other,” and “Romantic idealism” significantly predicted the depth subscale.”

    Study, 2021. Dost & Aras, Pegem Journal of Education and Instruction.

  3. The belief that partners must meet all needs of each other is linked to the social support subscale of relationship quality.

    ““We must be completely open and honest with each other,” “We must do everything together,” “We must meet all needs of each other,” and “Romantic idealism” significantly predicted the social support subscale of relationship quality”

    Study, 2021. Dost & Aras, Pegem Journal of Education and Instruction.

  4. Couples who engage in mutual emotional support have significantly lower levels of psychological distress than those with low dyadic engagement.

    “Furthermore, couples who engaged in mutual emotional support demonstrated significantly lower levels of psychological distress compared to those with low dyadic engagement.”

    Observational study, 2026. Nancy & Kazaara, Metropolitan Journal of Academic and Applied Research.

  5. Joint problemsolving is a significant predictor of resilience with a beta of 0.39 and a standard error of 0.07.

    “joint problemsolving (β = 0. 39, SE = 0. 07, p < . 001) were significant predictors of resilience”

    Observational study, 2026. Nancy & Kazaara, Metropolitan Journal of Academic and Applied Research.

Idealism and expectation exist in a delicate balance.

Romantic idealism is linked to relationship depth. However, the belief that one person must fulfill every need is linked to both social support and the conflict subscale.

Your search for the perfect person may be creating the friction you fear.

That may be why you feel constant tension. You may be looking for a total solution in another person, which can undermine the actual quality of the connection.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that romantic idealism and mutual emotional support are associated with positive relationship qualities, but specific idealistic beliefs about meeting all needs are linked to conflict.

The studies provided are observational and do not establish a causal link between these beliefs and relationship outcomes.
