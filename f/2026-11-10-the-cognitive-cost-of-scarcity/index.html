File no. 0263November 10, 2026Money and power

Poverty is linked to lower executive function at age 3.

High cortisol levels in children are associated with reduced ability to manage cognitive tasks.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Young people who have experienced poverty or homelessness are linked to deficits in working memory, general cognitive functioning, attention, and executive function.

    “General cognitive functioning, attention, and executive function deficits are shared by the homeless and poverty groups.”

    Systematic review, 2016. Fry et al., Child Neuropsychology.

  2. Higher levels of salivary cortisol in children are linked to lower executive function ability at age 3 years.

    “higher level of salivary cortisol assessed at ages 7, 15, and 24 months was uniquely associated with lower executive function ability”

    Observational study, 2011. Blair et al., Child Development.

  3. Financial scarcity is linked to a present time orientation which may decrease motivation for healthy dietary behavior.

    “Financial scarcity is related to a present time orientation, which affects dietary choices by shifting priorities and decreasing motivation for healthy dietary behavior.”

    Review, 2024. Veer et al., International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity.

  4. Early life stress is linked to deficits in cognitive performance, memory, and executive functioning.

    “Early adversity was found to be associated with deficits in a range of cognitive (cognitive performance, memory, and executive functioning”

    Review, 2011. Pechtel & Pizzagalli, Psychopharmacology.

  5. Cognitive load could decrease self-control capacity needed for healthy dietary behavior because it consumes mental bandwidth.

    “Cognitive load could decrease self-control capacity needed for healthy dietary behavior because it consumes mental bandwidth.”

    Review, 2024. Veer et al., International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity.

  6. Poverty is linked to a wide range of counterproductive economic behaviors.

    “Poverty is associated with a wide range of counterproductive economic behaviors.”

    Review, 2021. Bruijn & Antonides, Theory and Decision.

Scarcity consumes your mental bandwidth.

Financial stress is linked to a present time orientation. This reduces the mental capacity needed for long-term goals like healthy habits.

Your choices are not just about willpower.

That may be why making healthy decisions feels harder when your finances are unstable. The stress itself may be the barrier.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The honest summary

The sources confirm that poverty and scarcity are linked to reduced cognitive capacity and impaired decision-making, but they do not provide direct neuroimaging evidence of a specific brain change.

Several sources note that the evidence for scarcity theory is not conclusive and lacks precision, while others are observational rather than experimental.
