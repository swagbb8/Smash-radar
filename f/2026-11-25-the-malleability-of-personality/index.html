Are you actually who you think you are?

File no. 0332November 25, 2026Psychology

Your personality is not a fixed state.

Personality traits are both stable and changeable throughout your life span. Targeted interventions can produce measurable changes in your traits.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence backs this.

What the research found

  1. An intervention that trained participants to generate implementation intentions catalyzed their ability to attain trait changes.

    “a second intervention that trained participants to generate implementation intentions catalyzed their ability to attain trait changes.”

    Experiment, 2015. Hudson & Fraley, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

  2. Emotional stability was the primary trait domain showing changes as a result of therapy.

    “Emotional stability was the primary trait domain showing changes as a result of therapy, followed by extraversion.”

    Meta-analysis, 2017. Roberts et al., Psychological Bulletin.

  3. Patients presenting with anxiety disorders changed the most, and patients being treated for substance use changed the least.

    “Patients presenting with anxiety disorders changed the most, and patients being treated for substance use changed the least.”

    Meta-analysis, 2017. Roberts et al., Psychological Bulletin.

  4. Personality traits are both stable and changeable throughout the life span.

    “Past research syntheses provided evidence that personality traits are both stable and changeable throughout the life span.”

    Meta-analysis, 2022. Bleidorn et al., Psychological Bulletin.

Intentions drive measurable change.

Specific strategies like implementation intentions can catalyze your ability to attain trait changes. These changes are detectable by you and your social circle.

The limits of your nature.

That may be why you feel stuck in certain behaviors. The evidence suggests that your traits are more malleable than you believe.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A recurring reaction.

You sit in your car after a long shift and feel a familiar wave of irritation at a minor mistake. You believe this temper is simply who you are.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that personality is not fixed and can be significantly altered through targeted interventions, especially for those with specific goals.

The evidence on volitional personality change is still limited and requires more research to understand generalizability and mechanisms.
