Is your biology out of sync with your life?

File no. 0129October 20, 2026History

Industrialisation created a biological mismatch for humans.

Your biology is adapted for nature, but you live in an industrial world. This shift may have compromised human fitness.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

The honest summary
