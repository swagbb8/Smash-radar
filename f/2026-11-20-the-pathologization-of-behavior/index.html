Is your behavior a biological trait or a social regulation?

File no. 0308November 20, 2026History

Medicine used terms to judge womanhood.

Mid-twentieth century physicians used the term sexual frigidity to judge a woman's autonomy, character, and the success of her marriage.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Mid-twentieth century physicians used the term 'sexual frigidity' to judge women's autonomy, character, and the success of their marriages.

    “Through the frigidity narrative, mid-twentieth century physicians managed to pass judgment, not only on women’s sexuality, but on their autonomy, their character, and the success or failure of their marriage”

    Study, 2016. Margolin, Journal of Gender Studies.

  2. The discourse on 'sexual frigidity' was linked to how a woman performed as her husband's housekeeper and companion.

    “The discourse that seemed preoccupied with a woman’s genitals, with their feelings and contractions, was also interested in a woman’s heart, her intentions and activities, with particular emphasis on how she performed as her husband’s housekeeper and companion.”

    Study, 2016. Margolin, Journal of Gender Studies.

  3. Regulatory strategies for managing 'mean girls' are oriented toward maintaining appropriate modes of repressive, white, middle-class femininity.

    “Regulatory strategies emerging to manage mean girls are examined as oriented toward maintaining appropriate modes of repressive, white, middle-class femininity.”

    Study, 2006. Ringrose, Feminism & Psychology.

  4. Constructions of the 'mean girl' are linked to postfeminist gender anxieties over middle-class girl power and girl success.

    “Constructions of the mean girl are also linked to postfeminist gender anxieties over middle-class girl power and girl success.”

    Study, 2006. Ringrose, Feminism & Psychology.

  5. Contemporary Western culture frames psychological constructs in mental health in biomedical terms as dispositional within the suffering individual.

    “In contemporary Western culture, psychological constructs in the context of mental health have been framed in biomedical terms and understood as dispositional within the suffering individual.”

    Experiment, 2016. O’Reilly & Lester, Palgrave Macmillan UK eBooks.

  6. The number of published articles on social media in dermatology, dermatopathology, and pathology has increased since 2009.

    “The number of published articles on social media in these specialties has increased since 2009”

    Study, 2020. Saleh & Dahiya, Journal of Cutaneous Pathology.
