Does talking actually solve your problems?

File no. 0311November 21, 2026Human relationships

Some people report less rumination when they talk less.

Certain facets of alexithymia are linked to lower reports of rumination and social sharing.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. Certain facets of alexithymia are linked to lower reports of rumination and social sharing involving emotional aspects.

    “Certain facets of alexithymia were associated also with lower reports of rumination and social sharing involving emotional aspects”

    Study, 2004. Luminet et al., Cognition & Emotion.

  2. Individuals scoring high on the cognitive component of alexithymia are more prone to suppressive emotion regulation strategies rather than reappraisal strategies.

    “Individuals scoring high on the cognitive component of alexithymia are more prone to suppressive emotion regulation strategies rather than reappraisal strategies.”

    Study, 2009. Swart et al., PLoS ONE.

  3. Enhanced neural processing of intensity uniquely predicted increased tendency to subsequently choose distraction over reappraisal.

    “Results showed that enhanced neural processing of intensity (enhanced LPP amplitudes) uniquely predicted (above subjective measures of intensity) increased tendency to subsequently choose distraction over reappraisal.”

    Study, 2016. Shafir et al., Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience.

  4. Certain facets of alexithymia are linked to a lower proportion of emotional words related to the emotional stimulus during the verbal re-evocation.

    “with a lower proportion of emotional words related to the emotional stimulus during the verbal re-evocation.”

    Study, 2004. Luminet et al., Cognition & Emotion.

  5. The right hemisphere directly mediates the identification and comprehension of positive and negative emotional stimuli.

    “the right hemisphere (RH) directly mediates the identification and comprehension of positive and negative emotional stimuli”

    Review, 2014. Shobe, Frontiers in Human Neuroscience.

Processing varies by individual.

Individuals with high cognitive alexithymia may use suppression instead of reappraisal. Enhanced neural processing of intensity can predict a choice of distraction over reappraisal.

The mechanism is not universal.

That may be why your method of coping differs from others. The evidence does not show that talking reinforces negative pathways, but rather shows varied strategies for regulation.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A quiet room at night.

You sit on the edge of your bed. You think about your day but choose not to say a word to anyone. You prefer to let the thoughts pass without naming them.

The honest summary

The sources do not provide evidence that talking about problems reinforces negative neural pathways; instead, they show varied emotional processing and regulation strategies.

The studies on alexithymia and coping strategies are limited by small sample sizes or a high number of American-based samples.
