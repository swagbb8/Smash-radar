Is precision agriculture making our food more vulnerable?

File no. 0205October 31, 2026Technology

Precision agriculture does not show increased monoculture risk.

Current evidence focuses on using these technologies to improve crop resilience and sustainability rather than creating new vulnerabilities. The data does not show an acceleration of dominance for specific traits.

Supported. At least one solid peer-reviewed study. The evidence goes against the popular idea.

What the research found

  1. Mixed-species stands in production forestry are linked to reduced stand vulnerability to pest and pathogen damage.

    “However, some uncertainties and risks were projected to increase, highlighting the importance of conducting comprehensive interdisciplinary evaluations when assessing the pros and cons of mixtures.”

    Study, 2016. Felton et al., AMBIO. This shows a link, not a cause.

  2. The integration of genetic, biotechnological, and agronomic approaches demonstrates a significant potential to enhance crop resilience against abiotic and biotic stressors.

    “The integration of genetic, biotechnological, and agronomic approaches demonstrates a significant potential to enhance crop resilience against abiotic and biotic stressors while improving resource efficiency.”

    Review, 2024. Xing & Wang, Plants.

  3. Combining genetic selection and breeding with geospatial technologies (precision agriculture) may help enhance watermelon varieties for resistance to biotic and abiotic stress.

    “The present review discusses how genetic selection and breeding combined with geospatial technologies (precision agriculture) may help enhance watermelon varieties for resistance to biotic and abiotic stress.”

    Study, 2018. Kantor & Levi, Notulae Scientia Biologicae.

  4. Availability of and access to diverse genetic sources will ensure that the global food production network becomes more sustainable.

    “Since plant breeding research and cultivar development are integral components of improving food production, therefore, availability of and access to diverse genetic sources will ensure that the global food production network becomes more sustainable.”

    Review, 2015. Govindaraj et al., Genetics Research International.

  5. Improvements in the efficiency and precision of genetic technologies will enable a timely response to meet the multifaceted food requirements of a rapidly increasing world population.

    “Improvements in the efficiency and precision of genetic technologies will enable a timely response to meet the multifaceted food requirements of a rapidly increasing world population.”

    Study, 2010. Fahrenkrug et al., Journal of Animal Science.

Techniques aim to strengthen food security.

Combining genetic selection with geospatial technologies may help enhance varieties against stress. Access to diverse genetic sources is intended to make the global production network more sustainable.

The narrative of vulnerability is contested.

That may be why the fear of total crop failure from these methods is not supported by the current data. The evidence is mixed regarding the risks of these specific technologies.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The fruit on your kitchen table.

You eat a piece of fruit that was grown using these genetic and geospatial tools.

The honest summary

The sources focus on how precision agriculture and genetic diversity can improve crop resilience and sustainability rather than creating new vulnerabilities.

The sources do not contain specific data regarding the acceleration of genetic monoculture dominance or the specific risks of total crop failure from pathogens.
