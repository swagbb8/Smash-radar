Does your lack of sleep change your personality?

File no. 0017October 7, 2026The human brain

Sleep loss changes how your brain regulates emotion.

While sleep loss increases emotional reactivity, it also affects the brain's top-down emotion regulation in complex ways. The results regarding socio-emotional task performance are described as somewhat contradictory.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Selective REM sleep deprivation makes activity in the ventrolateral prefrontal cortex, which is involved in top-down emotion regulation, remain the same level or even increase compared to baseline.

    “In contrast, activity in these areas remained the same level or even increased in the REM-D group, compared to their BL level.”

    Experiment, 2012. Rosales-Lagarde et al., Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience.

  2. Sleep deprivation is linked to increased emotional reactivity, specifically regarding amygdala activity and its prefrontal regulation.

    “Sleep deprivation also increases emotional reactivity; results which are most apparent with neuro-imaging studies investigating amygdala activity and its prefrontal regulation.”

    Systematic review, 2014. Beattie et al., Sleep Medicine Reviews.

  3. Lack of REM sleep in humans is associated with enhanced emotional reactivity at both behavioral and neural levels.

    “Taken together, these results suggest that lack of REM sleep in humans is associated with enhanced emotional reactivity, both at behavioral and neural levels”

    Experiment, 2012. Rosales-Lagarde et al., Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience.

  4. Sleep deprivation is linked to diminished emotional expressivity and impaired emotion recognition.

    “From the reviewed literature, sleep deprivation is associated with diminished emotional expressivity and impaired emotion recognition, and this has particular relevance for social interactions.”

    Systematic review, 2014. Beattie et al., Sleep Medicine Reviews.

  5. Sleep loss is linked to a ~ 0.4% decrease for every hour awake prior to exercise for both deprivation and late-restriction protocols.

    “A significant positive relationship was observed between time awake prior to the exercise task and % Δ in performance for both deprivation and late-restriction protocols (~ 0. 4% decrease for every hour awake prior to exercise).”

    Meta-analysis, 2022. Craven et al., Sports Medicine.

Your brain loses its balance.

Lack of REM sleep is linked to enhanced emotional reactivity at both behavioral and neural levels. It is also linked to diminished emotional expressivity and impaired emotion recognition.

Your reactions are no longer your own.

That may be why you feel more reactive to your environment after a late night. Your brain may struggle to process and recognize social cues correctly.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

A sharp word in the kitchen.

You feel a surge of irritation at a small comment from a partner. You find it harder to read their face or calm your own response.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that sleep loss increases emotional reactivity and impairs social-emotional processing, but the specific neural mechanisms and effects on regulation can vary.

The results regarding the effects of sleep loss on socio-emotional task performance are described as somewhat contradictory.
