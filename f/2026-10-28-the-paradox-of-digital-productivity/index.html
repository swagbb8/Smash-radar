Is your software actually getting faster?

File no. 0191October 28, 2026Technology

Digital tools both hinder and help your output.

Instant communication disrupts deep work in academia, while AI tools improve software delivery and reduce manual effort. This evidence is mixed.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Unstructured use of generative artificial intelligence tools is linked to apparent performance without real competence development.

    “unstructured use tends to produce apparent performance without real competence development”

    Systematic review, 2026. Serpa & Ferreira, Brazilian Journal of Development.

  2. Instant digital communication tools are linked to a hidden toll on the ability of professors to manage their attention.

    “Work based on instant digital communication tools takes a hidden toll on the ability of professors to manage their attention.”

    Study, 2022. Ferreira, She ji.

  3. Instant communications among academic staff members are linked to the disruption of the deep work required for engagement in research and teaching.

    “Instant communications among academic staff members disrupts the deep work required for engagement in research and teaching.”

    Study, 2022. Ferreira, She ji.

  4. Snapchat communication cycles, such as notifications, Snap streaks and best friend rankings, are linked to workplace efficiency.

    “The focus is on analyzing how Snapchat’s communication cycles, such as notifications, Snap streaks and best friend rankings, affect workplace efficiency.”

    Study, 2024. Bilderback, Strategic HR Review.

  5. AI-powered development tools can significantly reduce manual effort, accelerate software delivery, and improve overall quality.

    “The findings show that AI-powered development tools can significantly reduce manual effort, accelerate software delivery, and improve overall quality”

    Study, 2025. Source, Computer Science Engineering and Technology.

The mechanics of modern work.

Constant digital messages link to a toll on attention and the disruption of deep research. Separately, AI-powered tools can accelerate software delivery and improve quality.

The shift in your focus.

That may be why your day feels fragmented by messages even as new tools automate your most tedious tasks. You are navigating two opposing forces.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The open tab.

You sit at your desk. A message notification blinks in the corner of your screen. You look at it. Your focus on the main project breaks for a second.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that while constant digital communication can hinder deep work, specific technological integrations like AI can simultaneously improve certain productivity metrics.

The research on communication disruptions is specific to academic settings, while the productivity gains from AI tools are specific to software development.
