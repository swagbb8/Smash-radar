Is your brain's idle mode a precursor to depression?

File no. 0254November 8, 2026The human brain

DMN connectivity does not provide a uniform biomarker for depression.

While these brain patterns are linked to rumination, the changes are highly variable and inconsistent across different people.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Trait rumination did not predict connectivity within and between DMN subsystems.

    “Trait rumination did not predict connectivity within and between DMN subsystems (all pFDR > 0. 05).”

    Meta-analysis, 2021. Tozzi et al., NeuroImage Clinical.

  2. Major depressive disorder shows slightly reduced functional connectivity within the DMN Core, but this effect is small, highly variable and heterogeneous.

    “We conclude that MDD as a diagnostic category shows slightly reduced functional connectivity within the DMN Core, independent of illness duration, treatment, symptoms and trait rumination.”

    Meta-analysis, 2021. Tozzi et al., NeuroImage Clinical.

  3. The Slow5 fSD DMN/SMN ratio is tilted toward the DMN in depression but shifted toward the SMN in mania.

    “The Slow5 DMN/SMN ratio was tilted toward the DMN in depression but was shifted toward the SMN in mania.”

    Study, 2016. Martino et al., Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences.

  4. Major depression is linked to greater connectivity variability between the mPFC and PCC.

    “Greater connectivity variability was detected in major depression between mPFC and PCC.”

    Study, 2017. Wise et al., Translational Psychiatry.

  5. Rumination is linked to activation in the DMN core regions and the dorsal medial prefrontal cortex subsystem.

    “Results confirm the suspected association between rumination and DMN activation, specifically implicating the DMN core regions and the dorsal medial prefrontal cortex subsystem.”

    Meta-analysis, 2019. Zhou et al., NeuroImage.

  6. In bipolar depression, the left cingulate gyrus and bilateral precuneus showed decreased ALFF/fractional ALFF.

    “BD patients showed specific changes in the DMN including decreased ALFF/fractional ALFF in the left cingulate gyrus (CG) and bilateral precuneus (PCUN)”

    Meta-analysis, 2023. Xue et al., Journal of Affective Disorders.

The brain's default mode shows mixed signals.

Major depression is linked to greater connectivity variability between the mPFC and PCC. However, the effect size is small and heterogeneous.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that DMN alterations are associated with depression and rumination, but the effects are inconsistent and do not provide a simple, uniform biomarker.

The small effect size and high variability of DMN connectivity changes limit their use as reliable clinical or prognostic markers.
