The uncomfortable truth

Why does a lie reach you before the truth?

File no. 0101October 16, 2026Social media

Falsehoods diffuse farther and faster than truth.

False news spreads more broadly and deeper than the truth in every category of information. You are moving through a landscape where lies travel with more speed than facts.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence backs this.

What the research found

  1. Robots accelerated the spread of true and false news at the same rate.

    “Contrary to conventional wisdom, robots accelerated the spread of true and false news at the same rate, implying that false news spreads more than the truth because humans, not robots, are more likely to spread it.”

    Study, 2018. Vosoughi et al., Science.

  2. Participants were better at rating false news as false than at rating true news as true.

    “We found that people rated true news as more accurate than false news (Cohen’s d = 1.12 [1.01, 1.22]) and were better at rating false news as false than at rating true news as true (Cohen’s d = 0.32 [0.24, 0.39]).”

    Meta-analysis, 2025. Pfänder & Altay, Nature Human Behaviour.

  3. False news was more novel than true news.

    “We found that false news was more novel than true news, which suggests that people were more likely to share novel information.”

    Study, 2018. Vosoughi et al., Science.

  4. User interactions with false content rose steadily on both Facebook and Twitter through the end of 2016, but have since fallen sharply on Facebook while continuing to rise on Twitter.

    “User interactions with false content rose steadily on both Facebook and Twitter through the end of 2016. Since then, however, interactions with false content have fallen sharply on Facebook while continuing to rise on Twitter”

    Study, 2019. Allcott et al., Research & Politics.

  5. Falsehood diffused significantly farther, faster, deeper, and more broadly than the truth in all categories of information.

    “Falsehood diffused significantly farther, faster, deeper, and more broadly than the truth in all categories of information”

    Study, 2018. Vosoughi et al., Science.

  6. Participants who paused to explain why a headline was true or false indicated that they were less likely to share false information compared to control participants.

    “In an online experiment, participants who paused to explain why a headline was true or false indicated that they were less likely to share false information compared to control participants.”

    Experiment, 2020. Fazio, Harvard Kennedy School Misinformation Review.

Novelty drives the reach of lies.

False news is more novel than true news. Humans are more likely to share information that is new or surprising. This behavior allows falsehoods to travel further than the truth.

Your attention is a target.

That may be why your feed feels overwhelming. You are wired to notice the new and the surprising. Falsehoods exploit this biological tendency to reach you before the truth can.

This part is our reading of the evidence, not a finding.

The scroll at 11 p.m.

You lie in bed. You swipe your thumb across the glass. A headline about a disaster appears. You share it before you check the source because it feels urgent and new. You have participated in the speed of a lie.

The honest summary

The evidence confirms that false news spreads more effectively than truth due to human behavior and novelty, though people are generally capable of discerning falsehoods.

While false news spreads more broadly, its relative magnitude on Facebook has declined since 2016 while continuing to rise on Twitter.
