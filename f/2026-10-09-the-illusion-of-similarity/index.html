Why do we choose people who are different?

File no. 0054October 9, 2026Human relationships

Perceived similarity matters more than actual similarity.

In existing relationships, how similar you think your partner is predicts attraction more than how similar you actually are.

Established. Several studies combined, or official data. The evidence is mixed.

What the research found

  1. Actual similarity is not significant in predicting attraction in existing relationships.

    “the effect of actual similarity in existing relationships was not significant.”

    Meta-analysis, 2008. Montoya et al., Journal of Social and Personal Relationships.

  2. Perceived similarity predicts attraction in no-interaction, short-interaction, and existing relationship studies.

    “Alternatively, perceived similarity predicted attraction in no-interaction, short-interaction, and existing relationship studies.”

    Meta-analysis, 2008. Montoya et al., Journal of Social and Personal Relationships.

  3. Genetic similarity between partners was found for educational attainment (r g = 0.37), height (r g = 0.13), and depression (r g = 0.08).

    “We found evidence of genetic similarity between partners for educational attainment ( r g = 0. 37), height ( r g = 0. 13), and depression ( r g = 0. 08).”

    Observational study, 2022. Torvik et al., Nature Communications.

  4. Greater disagreement between women and men's erratic lifestyle (ELS) is linked with a sharper decrease of women's relationship quality.

    “Greater disagreement between women and men's ELS was related with more sharp decrease of women's relationship quality”

    Experiment, 2017. Kardum et al., Psihologijske teme.

  5. Greater disagreement between women and men's interpersonal manipulation (IM) results in a lower women's relationship quality.

    “Greater disagreement between women and men's IM results in a lower women's relationship quality”

    Experiment, 2017. Kardum et al., Psihologijske teme.

  6. Actual similarity is linked to interpersonal attraction with a correlation of r = .47.

    “Results indicated that the associations between interpersonal attraction and both actual similarity (r = .47) and perceived similarity (r = .39) were significant and large.”

    Meta-analysis, 2008. Montoya et al., Journal of Social and Personal Relationships.

The evidence is mixed.

While actual similarity correlates with attraction, it is not always a significant predictor in existing bonds. However, specific disagreements in traits like lifestyle or manipulation can lower relationship quality.

The honest summary

The evidence shows that while assortative mating leads to similarities in traits like education and height, the psychological attraction in long-term relationships depends more on how similar people perceive each other to be.

One study shows that actual similarity is not a significant predictor of attraction in existing relationships, while another shows that disagreement in certain personality components can decrease relationship quality.
