# MONKEY'S LIFE — the whole story (Ash's words, pasted Oct 24 2026)

Monkey's Life is a dark sci-fi mystery comedy about a monkey named Seven who wakes up alone in a swamp in a world where humans are
gone and only animals remain. He has no memory of his past, no idea how he got there, and no explanation for the futuristic device
fused into his arm.

In reality, Seven came from a future where humans were still alive. He was being tested in a laboratory, where scientists studied
his intelligence, memory, and ability to learn. They taught him about human inventions and concepts using pictures, newspapers,
objects, puzzles, and simulations. During a moment of panic, Seven started smashing things and accidentally damaged the device on his
arm. The malfunction sent him far back in time, into a world before human civilization, while wiping out most of his memories.

As Seven explores this strange world, his damaged device begins causing random memory fogs. He sees flashes of humans, laboratory
experiments, newspaper headlines, and inventions from the future. Sometimes he remembers a word without knowing what it means. Other
times, he sees an image that gives him an idea he can't explain. He gradually learns English, often in hilarious ways, and starts
teaching words and concepts to his tribe.

Eventually, Seven uses these broken memories to help his tribe advance. He recreates things humans once invented, starting with
simple tools such as fishing poles and progressing toward light bulbs, engines, cars, airplanes, and more. His tribe begins evolving
socially and technologically, and the other animal tribes may follow their own paths as knowledge spreads. The comedy comes from
monkeys interpreting human ideas in ridiculous ways, while the mystery deepens as Seven uncovers more of his forgotten past.

The bigger mystery is why Seven was being tested in the first place. As his memories return and the device becomes increasingly
unstable, he begins to realize that the experiments may have had a purpose far beyond teaching him. The truth about the laboratory,
the device, and humanity's fate could change everything he believes about the world.

The opening scene — Seven wakes up covered in mud in the swamp, struggles to climb a tree, and discovers the shattered device fused
to his arm — is where the audience first enters this world.

Core idea: the rise of a new civilization through the eyes of one confused monkey who unknowingly carries fragments of humanity's
knowledge, while slowly uncovering a sci-fi mystery about his own existence.

Still open (not established by Ash): the laboratory's ultimate purpose and exactly what happened to humanity.
