Settles on Chainlink's BTC/USD 60-second TWAP at close versus the open. Win chance is a math model from recent volatility, Coinbase's lead and time left. EV is your average result on the stake if the win chance is right, using the price you'd pay right now (best ask), before any fees. It's an estimate, not a sure thing; fast traders close gaps quickly. Price to beat is exact only if the page was open when the window started. Polymarket may be restricted where you live.
